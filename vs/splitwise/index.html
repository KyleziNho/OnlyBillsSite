Questions

Doesn’t Splitwise do this now?

Splitwise added receipt splitting in October 2026, and it’s a good step. But you still need a Splitwise account, UK friends settle through Pay by Bank or cash, there’s no receipt translation, and Pro costs £39.99 a year. OnlyBills needs no account, gets you paid straight to your bank tonight and costs £5.99 a year.

Is OnlyBills cheaper than Splitwise Pro?

Yes, nearly 7 times cheaper. OnlyBills is £5.99 a year or £0.99 a month. Splitwise Pro is £39.99 a year or £3.99 a month on the UK App Store, so a whole year of OnlyBills costs less than two months of Pro. Your first split is free.

Do my friends need an account or the app?

No. Friends scan your QR code or open your link, type their name and tick what they had, in their browser or as an App Clip on iPhone. Only the person who paid needs OnlyBills, and even they don’t make an account.

Can I use OnlyBills and Splitwise together?

Plenty of people do. Splitwise for the flat’s rent and bills, OnlyBills for dinners and nights out, where everyone had something different.

How do friends pay me?

With a Monzo, Starling, Revolut or PayPal link that already has their exact share in it, or by bank transfer. Monzo links take card, Apple Pay or bank transfer with no Monzo account needed. The money goes straight to you.
