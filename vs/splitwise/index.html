Questions

Doesn’t Splitwise do this now?

Splitwise added receipt splitting in October 2026. But you still need a Splitwise account, UK friends settle through Pay by Bank or cash, there’s no receipt translation, and Pro costs £39.99 a year. OnlyBills needs no account, gets you paid straight to your bank tonight and is free.

How much does each one cost?

OnlyBills is free, with no sign-up. Free includes 30 receipt scans in any 30 days. Typing a bill in is always unlimited. OnlyBills Pro, optional, raises the scan limit. Splitwise is free with a daily cap on new expenses, and Splitwise Pro is £39.99 a year (or £3.99 a month) on the UK App Store.

Do my friends need an account or the app?

No. They scan your QR code, type their name and tick what they had, in their browser or as an App Clip on iPhone. You don’t make an account either.

How do friends pay me?

With a Monzo, Starling, Revolut or PayPal link that already has their exact share in it, or by bank transfer. Monzo links take card, Apple Pay or bank transfer, no Monzo account needed. The money goes straight to you.

Can I use both?

Plenty of people do. Splitwise for the flat’s rent and bills, OnlyBills for dinners and nights out, where everyone had something different.
