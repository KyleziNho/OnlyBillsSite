Month to month, OnlyBills is £0.99 against £4.99 for Splitwise Pro: five times cheaper. On the yearly plan it works out at about 50p a month. Your first split is free, there's a free trial, and the friends who join your table never pay anything.

What you get with OnlyBills that you don't with Splitwise

Paid back to the bank you already use. Each friend sees their exact amount and taps to pay you. Monzo links take card, Apple Pay or bank transfer, with no Monzo account needed. Starling and Revolut links take card or Apple Pay, no account needed. PayPal (an account may be needed) and plain bank transfer from any banking app work too. The money goes straight to you.

An App Clip on iPhone. Friends scan your QR code and the table opens straight away as an App Clip, no download. On any other phone it opens in the browser.

Receipts in any language. Scan a receipt in Thai, Chinese or Spanish and every dish comes through in English automatically, so everyone knows what they're ticking. Handy on holiday. (You can turn it off in Settings.)

Owed. Every bill you host lands in one place, with your friends' faces floating around what you're owed. Add an IOU for anything else (the taxi, the concert tickets) and OnlyBills keeps a running balance with each person.

When Splitwise might suit you better

If you share a house and split rent and bills every month, or want an app on Android, Splitwise is built for that. Plenty of people use both: Splitwise for the house, OnlyBills for dinners out.

Questions people ask

Is OnlyBills cheaper than Splitwise Pro?

Yes. OnlyBills is £0.99 a month or £5.99 a year. Splitwise Pro is £4.99 a month, so a whole year of OnlyBills costs about the same as one month of Pro.

Can Splitwise split a receipt by item?

Yes. Splitwise's Itemize lets one person photograph a receipt and friends claim their items from a QR code. OnlyBills does this too, and adds UK bank pay links, an App Clip and receipt translation.

Does OnlyBills track running balances like Splitwise?

Yes. The Owed page keeps a running balance with each friend across every bill you host, and you can add IOUs for anything else.

Do friends need an account for OnlyBills?

No. Friends join from a link or QR code in their browser, or an App Clip on iPhone, type their name and tick their items. Only the person who paid needs the app.
