How to split a bill when everyone uses a different payment app

Venmo, Cash App, Zelle, PayPal or Apple Cash? Who needs which account, which ones work without one, and one message that lets every friend pay you back.

Kyle, founder of OnlyBillsNovember 14, 2026 · 5 min read

When friends use different payment apps, don't try to get everyone onto yours. List two or three ways you can be paid, give each friend their exact amount, and let them pick. A friend without Venmo can still use Zelle from their bank app, a Cash App payment link with Apple Pay, or PayPal.

The useful thing to know is which methods need your friend to have an account, and which don't. That's the table below. After it: mixed groups in Europe, friends visiting from abroad, and one message you can paste into the group chat.

US payment apps: who needs what

Checked against each company's help pages and terms on 14 November 2026.

Method Does your friend need an account? Can you send a link? Amount filled in?
Venmo Yes, a Venmo account Yes, your profile link Yes in a request you send; a profile link doesn't carry one
Cash App No, for a payment link made in Cash App: they can pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay (US, 18+) Yes Yes, in a request or payment link you create
Zelle Yes, Zelle in their US bank or credit union app No Zelle link exists No, they type it
PayPal Usually a PayPal account Yes, a PayPal.Me link Yes, if you add the amount to the link
Apple Cash Yes, Apple Cash on an iPhone, US only No, it works inside Messages Yes, in a Messages request

Two things the table hides:

  • Venmo and PayPal now talk to each other, a little. PayPal users in the US can send money to a Venmo account by searching your Venmo phone number. Requests don't cross between the two, so they have to send it.
  • Cash App and Venmo don't. There's no way to move money directly from one to the other.

The simplest setup for a mixed group

Pick a main method and one or two backups. For most US groups that's:

  1. Venmo or Cash App as the main one, since most friends will have one of them.
  2. Zelle as the backup, because nearly every big bank has it and nobody needs a new app.
  3. A Cash App payment link for the friend who has neither, since they can pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay.

Then do the math once and send everyone the same message with their own number in it. If people ordered different things, split by item with the tax and tip formula first.

Mixed groups in Europe

The same idea works abroad, with different apps:

  • A Revolut payment link can be paid by card or Apple Pay, even by friends who don't use Revolut.
  • An IBAN works for everyone with a European bank account. Instant euro transfers are now standard across the eurozone.
  • Local phone-number apps like Bizum in Spain, MB WAY in Portugal or Swish in Sweden are quick for locals, but a visitor usually can't use them without a local bank.

So a group with a Spaniard, a German and an American might use Bizum for one, an IBAN transfer for another, and Revolut or PayPal for the third.

Friends visiting from another country

Venmo, Cash App, Zelle and Apple Cash are all built for paying people inside the US. A friend visiting from London or Madrid probably can't use any of them. What usually works across borders:

  • PayPal, if you both have it. Watch the currency conversion fee.
  • Wise or Revolut, which are built for sending money abroad and show the rate before you send.
  • Cash at the table, for small amounts.

Agree on the currency first. If the bill was in euros and you want dollars, settle the rate before anyone pays.

One message that covers everyone

Paste this into the group chat, with your own details:

Dinner's split by what everyone ordered (tax and tip included): Maya $22.91 · Jordan $53.44 · Sam $31.18 Pay whichever way works for you: Venmo @your-name · Cash App $yourname · Zelle (555) 010-0100 Thanks!

Every friend sees their own number and a method they already have. Nobody has to ask "what's your Venmo?" in the chat.

Questions people ask

What if my friend doesn't have Venmo?

Give them another way to pay you. Zelle works from most US bank apps, a Cash App payment link can be paid with Apple Pay or Google Pay without a Cash App account, and PayPal users in the US can now send money to a Venmo account.

Can you send money from Cash App to Venmo?

No. Cash App and Venmo are separate networks, so money can't go directly from one to the other. Your friend pays you on whichever app you both have, or by another method like Zelle.

What's the easiest way to pay someone in another country?

Usually an app you both already use that works across borders, such as PayPal, Wise or Revolut. Venmo, Cash App, Zelle and Apple Cash are US-only for paying friends. Check the fee and exchange rate before you send.

Which app is best for splitting a bill?

The one your whole group already has. If everyone's on Venmo, Venmo's split and receipt scanning do the job. If the group is mixed, list two or three ways you accept money and send each friend their exact amount.
