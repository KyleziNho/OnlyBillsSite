The best bill splitting apps in 2026

Venmo, Cash App, Apple Cash, Zelle, Splitwise, Tricount, Settle Up and OnlyBills compared: who each suits, what's free, and who needs an account.

The best bill splitting app is usually the one your friends already have. If everyone uses Venmo, its split and new receipt scanning cover most dinners. For a trip or a shared apartment, use a balance app like Splitwise, Tricount or Settle Up. For one restaurant check where everyone ordered different things and not everyone is on the same payment app, use something that lets each person claim their own items, like Splitwise Itemize or OnlyBills.

Below: which app fits which job, what each one does well, what's free, and who needs an account. We checked every fact on the company's own site, help pages or US App Store listing on 14 November 2026. Apps change quickly, so check the listing before you pay for anything. (In the UK? See the best bill splitting apps in the UK.)

Venmo

Venmo is where most US friends already settle up, so it's often the path of least resistance. You can split a purchase you made with Venmo, or tap the blue V button to split any other bill by amount. On 9 November 2026 Venmo announced receipt scanning: photograph the receipt, assign items, and Venmo adds each person's share of tax and tip and sends the requests. It's rolling out over the following weeks. Venmo Groups keeps a running balance for up to 30 people for trips and roommates.

Limits: everyone needs a Venmo account to pay. Credit card payments cost 3%. Full steps in how to split a bill on Venmo.

Cash App

Cash App has no split button, but it has the best answer to "my friend doesn't use it": a request sent as a link can be paid with Apple Pay or Google Pay without a Cash App account. Pools collect money toward a goal from a group, with the same Apple Pay and Google Pay option.

Limits: one request per person, and it doesn't work out who owes what. More in how to split a bill on Cash App.

Apple Cash

Apple added Split Bill to Apple Cash. You scan a receipt in Messages, Wallet, Camera or Photos, check the items, add a tip, and split by item or evenly. Friends open the request in Messages, pick their items and pay with Apple Cash.

Limits: US only, everyone needs Apple Cash on an iPhone or iPad, and receipt scanning needs a device with Apple Intelligence. Great for an all-iPhone group, no use for the friend on Android.

Zelle, through your bank

Zelle has no app of its own any more. It lives inside more than 2,400 bank and credit union apps, and some banks add a split: Chase lets you split a charge and include your own portion, and U.S. Bank splits with up to 10 people.

Limits: requests only reach people already enrolled, and there's no Zelle payment link, so friends type your number or email into their bank app. See can you split a bill with Zelle?

Splitwise

Splitwise is the best known balance app. You add expenses as they happen, split them equally, by amount, percentage or shares, and it keeps a running balance per group on iPhone, Android and the web. In October 2026 it launched Itemize in the US and UK: one person photographs the receipt, the others scan a QR code and claim their items with no download or account, and tax and tip are shared in proportion. Itemize is free.

Free or paid: free, with a daily cap on new expenses. Splitwise Pro adds currency conversion, receipt storage, search and charts, among other things. The US App Store lists Pro purchases from $2.99 to $39.99.

Suits: groups who'll keep using it. For how it compares on one restaurant check, see OnlyBills vs Splitwise.

Tricount

Tricount is the free one. Its App Store listing promises "no subscription, no hidden fees, no limit on tricounts or expenses", supports multiple currencies for trips, and lets you share what friends owe you so they can settle without being on the app.

Limits: it doesn't read receipts line by line. You add each expense as an amount. More in Tricount alternatives.

Settle Up

Settle Up tracks shared costs for trips, roommates and couples, supports all currencies with live exchange rates, works offline, and lets friends join from a link or QR code and view balances in a browser.

Free or paid: free with ads. Premium removes ads and adds receipt photos, recurring expenses and statistics: $3.99 a month or $19.99 a year on the US App Store, or you can upgrade one group from $5.99 for a week.

OnlyBills

OnlyBills does one job: splitting a restaurant or bar check by what each person had, when the group doesn't all use the same app. The host scans the receipt and it reads every line, including tax and tip. Friends scan a QR code or open a link and tick their own items in the browser or an App Clip, with no download or sign-up, and watch their share add up live. Shared dishes split between the people who shared them, and tax and tip are spread in proportion, exact to the cent.

Then each friend sees their share and your ways to be paid: Venmo or Cash App (they enter the amount when it opens), Zelle (they copy your number and the amount into their bank app) or PayPal. Owed keeps track of who still owes you across checks.

Free or paid: free, with no sign-up for anyone, host included. Free includes 30 receipt scans in any 30 days. Typing a bill in is always unlimited. OnlyBills Pro, optional, raises the scan limit.

Not for: a whole trip's running costs or rent (use Splitwise, Tricount or Settle Up), and there's no Android app for the host, though Android friends can join from their browser.

Does itemizing make a real difference?

On an uneven check, yes. Four friends, $142.60 of food and drink, 8.875% tax and a 20% tip: $183.78 in total.

Questions people ask

What's the best free bill splitting app?

It depends on the job. For one restaurant check split by item: Venmo's receipt scanning (rolling out), Apple Cash's Split Bill, Splitwise Itemize and OnlyBills are all free. For a trip or a shared house over months: Tricount is free with no subscription, and Splitwise and Settle Up have free plans.

Does Splitwise have a free version?

Yes. Splitwise is free to use, with a daily cap on how many new expenses you can add. Its Itemize receipt splitting is free in the US and UK. Splitwise Pro, which is paid, adds features such as currency conversion and lifts the expense limit.

Which app splits a receipt by item?

Venmo (receipt scanning, rolling out from November 2026), Apple Cash Split Bill in Messages (needs Apple Intelligence to scan), Splitwise Itemize and OnlyBills. Tricount and Settle Up split amounts, not receipts.

Do my friends need to download the app?

For Venmo, Apple Cash and Zelle, friends need an account to pay. Cash App payment links and Pools can be paid with Apple Pay or Google Pay. Splitwise Itemize and OnlyBills let friends claim items from a QR code without downloading anything.
