How to split a bill with Interac e-Transfer

Work out each share, then use Request Money in your bank app, one request per friend. Some banks split for you. Fees, timing and request scams to avoid.

To split a bill with Interac e-Transfer, work out each person's share and send each friend a Request Money from your banking app, one request per person. Some banks have a split option built in: BMO's SplitShare, for example, lets you ask up to 10 contacts at once.

Below are the steps, a worked example with tax and tip, what it costs, and the request scams to watch for.

How to request money with Interac e-Transfer

Request Money works like a normal e-Transfer in reverse. Interac describes the steps like this:

  1. Open your banking app or online banking and find Request Money (usually in the e-Transfer section).
  2. Choose the friend. If it's your first request to them, add them with their email address or mobile number.
  3. Enter the amount and, if you have more than one, the account the money should go into.
  4. Add a message, such as "Saturday dinner: your share".

Your friend gets a notification and pays from their own bank. Interac says the request sends reminders if needed, and payments are "typically almost instant, but can take up to 30 minutes". You can cancel a request while it's still pending.

Not every bank offers Request Money, and your friend's bank has to take part too. Interac keeps a list of participating institutions.

Does any bank split the bill for you?

Some do. BMO's SplitShare, in the BMO mobile app, lets you divide a cost and request payment from up to 10 contacts. Other banks may have a similar option, so check your app's e-Transfer menu. Otherwise, send one request per person.

Either way, the bank divides by whatever amounts you enter. If people ordered different things, you need to work out the shares first.

Worked example: dinner in Toronto

Three friends, one bill. Liam paid. Ontario's HST is 13%, and the table agreed an 18% tip on the pre-tax subtotal.

Maya Liam Priya Total
Food and drink $32.00 $48.00 $40.00 $120.00
HST 13% $4.16 $6.24 $5.20 $15.60
Tip 18% of pre-tax $5.76 $8.64 $7.20 $21.60
Share $41.92 $62.88 $52.40 $157.20

Liam sends two requests: $41.92 to Maya and $52.40 to Priya. Splitting $157.20 three ways would have charged Maya $52.40, about $10 more than she ate.

The quick method: add up tax and tip ($37.20), divide by the subtotal (31%), and add that percentage to everyone's own total. More on tipping norms in tipping and service charge by country.

What does it cost, and are there limits?

Interac says there may be a fee from your bank or credit union for Request Money. Some charge per request, some take it from the money you receive, and some accounts include e-Transfers in the monthly plan. Limits per transfer and per day are also set by each bank. Check your bank's fee page before you send ten requests.

Request Money scams to watch for

Request Money asks someone to send you money. Scammers use that:

  • A request from a stranger, or from a "company" saying you owe them. Ignore it, and contact the company through its own website if you're unsure.
  • "Accept this to receive your refund." Paying a request always sends money. You never pay to receive.
  • A request that doesn't match what you agreed. Check the amount and the sender's name.

You can opt out of requests from a particular person, or from the service, in your bank's e-Transfer settings.

Visiting from outside Canada?

Interac e-Transfer needs a Canadian bank account. A friend visiting from abroad can pay you by PayPal if you both have it, or you can agree a rate and settle in their currency later. See splitting a bill abroad.

Questions people ask

Can you split a bill with Interac e-Transfer?

Yes. Work out each person's share, then use Request Money in your banking app to send each friend a request for their amount. Some banks, such as BMO with SplitShare, can request from several people at once.

Does Interac Request Money cost anything?

It depends on your bank or credit union. Interac says there may be a fee, and some charge one per request while others include it in your account plan. Check your bank's fee page.

How long does an Interac e-Transfer take?

Interac says it's typically almost instant, but can take up to 30 minutes depending on the bank.

Is a Request Money message safe?

A genuine request asks you to send money. If a request arrives from someone you don't know, or one claims you'll receive money by accepting it, ignore it. Pay only requests from friends you were expecting to hear from.
