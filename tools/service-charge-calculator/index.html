Service charge calculator

Add 10, 12.5 or 15% to a bill, or work backwards from what you paid to see how much was service.

Service charge

How to work out service charge

Adding it: multiply the bill by the percentage. 12.5% of £75 is £75 × 0.125 = £9.38 (rounded to the penny), so the total is £84.38.

Finding it in a total: divide the total by 1 plus the percentage. If you paid £84.38 including 12.5% service, the bill before service was £84.38 ÷ 1.125 = £75.00, so £9.38 of what you paid was service.

Is service charge optional?

A discretionary or optional service charge is exactly that: you can ask for it to be taken off. Read is service charge mandatory in the UK? for when a charge can be compulsory and what to say if you want it removed.

Splitting service charge in a group

The fairest way is in proportion to what each person had: everyone adds the same percentage to their own share. The split the bill calculator does it item by item, and this guide explains why it beats splitting the service equally.

Questions people ask

How much is 12.5% service charge on £100?

£12.50, making the bill £112.50. If £100 is the total you paid including 12.5% service, the service part was £11.11.

How do I take service charge off a total?

Divide the total by 1.125 for 12.5% service (or 1.1 for 10%, 1.15 for 15%). That gives you the bill before service. The difference is the service charge.

Is service charge the same as a tip?

In practice, yes. A discretionary service charge is a tip added to the bill for you. Since October 2024, employers in Great Britain must pass it all to staff. You don't need to tip on top.

Is service charge added before or after a discount?

Usually after: the percentage is worked out on the bill once any discount has come off. Check the receipt if you're not sure.
