Tip or service

How much should you tip in the UK?

In UK restaurants, 10 to 12.5% is normal for table service, and many restaurants add a 12.5% "discretionary" service charge to the bill. If it's already there, you don't need to tip again. In pubs and for counter service, tipping isn't expected. For a full guide by situation, see how much to tip in the UK.

Do staff get the service charge?

Since 1 October 2024, the Employment (Allocation of Tips) Act 2023 means employers in England, Scotland and Wales must pass all tips and service charges to workers, fairly and without deductions. More in is service charge mandatory in the UK?

Splitting the tip when people ordered different amounts

If one person had a £12 pasta and another had £45 of steak and wine, giving the tip in equal parts isn't quite fair. Each person adding the same percentage to what they had is. The split the bill calculator does this for you.

Questions people ask

Is 12.5% service charge normal in the UK?

Yes. 12.5% is the most common service charge in UK restaurants, especially in cities. It's discretionary, which means optional.

Should I tip if service charge is included?

No need. The service charge is the tip. Only add more if the service was exceptional and you want to.

Do you tip on the total including drinks?

Usually, yes. A service charge is normally worked out on the whole bill, food and drink.

How do I split a tip between friends?

The fairest way is for everyone to add the same percentage to what they had. Splitting the tip equally is fine when everyone spent about the same.
