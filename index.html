Questions

Do my friends need to download OnlyBills?

No. When you share a live table, friends scan the QR code or open the link and claim their items in their browser (or as an App Clip on iPhone). They can pay you from there too. Only the person hosting the bill needs the app.

How much does OnlyBills cost?

Your first split is free. After that it’s £0.99 a month or £5.99 a year, with a free trial. You can cancel any time in your iPhone’s Settings.

How do people pay me back?

You add how you’d like to be paid: a Monzo, Starling or Revolut payment link, PayPal, or your bank details. Friends tap to pay their exact share. Monzo links take card, Apple Pay or bank transfer with no Monzo account needed; Starling and Revolut take card or Apple Pay; bank transfer works from any banking app.

Does OnlyBills take a cut of the money?

No. Payments go straight from your friends to you through your bank or PayPal. OnlyBills never holds your money.

What about service charge, tips and discounts?

They’re spread across everyone in proportion to what they had, and shared dishes split between the people who shared them. Every share is exact to the penny and they add up to the bill.

Can friends with Android phones join?

Yes. The live table opens in any modern phone browser, so friends on Android can claim their items and pay. The OnlyBills app itself is for iPhone and iPad.

How is OnlyBills different from Splitwise?

Both can split a receipt by item. OnlyBills adds Monzo, Starling and Revolut pay links with each friend’s exact share, an App Clip on iPhone and automatic receipt translation. It needs no sign-up at all, and a whole year costs £5.99 against £39.99 for Splitwise Pro.

What if the receipt is read wrong?

You check every receipt before you share it. OnlyBills shows whether the items add up to the total, and you can edit any line, price or quantity.
