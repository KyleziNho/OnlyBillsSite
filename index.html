How it works

Three steps. About a minute.

  1. 1

    Snap the receipt

    OnlyBills reads every line and the service charge, and translates it if it isn’t in English. Check it, fix anything, done.

  2. 2

    Share one QR code

    Choose “Everyone taps their own”. Friends scan it and tick what they had, in their browser or the App Clip.

  3. 3

    Get paid back

    Everyone pays their exact share straight to you, by Monzo, Starling, Revolut, PayPal or bank transfer.

Made for the end of the meal.

Your friends don’t need the app.

They open the table in their browser or the App Clip, add their name and tap what they had. iPhone or Android, no account.

Questions

Do my friends need to download OnlyBills?

No. When you share a live table, friends scan the QR code or open the link and claim their items in their browser (or as an App Clip on iPhone). They can pay you from there too. Only the person hosting the bill needs the app.

How much does OnlyBills cost?

It’s free. There’s no sign-up, no ads and no trial to cancel, and friends who join your table never pay anything. Receipt scanning has a fair-use limit of 30 scans in 30 days, and manual entry is always unlimited. OnlyBills Pro, optional, gives higher scan limits and supports the app. Nothing is locked behind it.

How do people pay me back?

You add how you’d like to be paid: a Monzo, Starling or Revolut payment link, PayPal, or your bank details. Friends tap to pay their exact share. Monzo links take card, Apple Pay or bank transfer with no Monzo account needed; Starling and Revolut take card or Apple Pay; bank transfer works from any banking app.

Does OnlyBills take a cut of the money?

No. Payments go straight from your friends to you through your bank or PayPal. OnlyBills never holds your money.

Can friends with Android phones join?

Yes. The live table opens in any modern phone browser, so friends on Android can claim their items and pay. The OnlyBills app itself is for iPhone and iPad.

How is OnlyBills different from Splitwise?

Both can split a receipt by item. OnlyBills adds Monzo, Starling and Revolut pay links with each friend’s exact share, an App Clip on iPhone and automatic receipt translation. It needs no sign-up at all, and it’s free, where Splitwise Pro is £39.99 a year.
