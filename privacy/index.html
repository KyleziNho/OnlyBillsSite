Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 30 October 2026

OnlyBills is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app, including when receipt scanning, Live Split, venue lookups, bill link previews, feedback notes and usage analytics use secure third-party cloud services.

Information We Collect

OnlyBills is designed with privacy in mind. We do not run our own accounts system and we do not collect personal information for advertising or profiling.

The app accesses, with your permission:

  • • Your device camera to scan receipts
  • • Your photo library to import receipt images
  • • Your contacts to help you split bills with friends

Some features send data to secure third-party cloud services to work:

  • • Receipt scanning uploads the receipt image to Google's Gemini API for analysis
  • • Live Split stores the receipt's items, the display names people enter, who claimed what, and who has marked themselves as paid in Google Firebase so guests can join and split in real time. Receipt images shared during Live Split may be stored in Firebase Storage.
  • • When you share a live table, the payment links and bank details you have chosen to show (for example your Monzo username, or your account name, sort code and account number) are included so guests can pay you. They are visible to anyone with the table link.
  • • To show a restaurant's name and photo, the venue name and address read from your receipt are sent to Google Places. Nothing about you or your friends is included.
  • • When someone opens a bill link, or an app like Messages or WhatsApp shows a preview of it, the request goes through Cloudflare, which serves our website. For a bill link, Cloudflare reads that table's venue name and photo so the preview can show them. Anyone with the link can already see these on the bill page.
  • • If you send us a note from the app or a bill page, we store your message, the screen you sent it from, your app and iOS version and device model, and, only if you give it, the email or number you'd like a reply on.
  • • Receipt scanning has a fair-use limit. To apply it, the app keeps a count of your recent scans on your device. The count is not sent to us and is not linked to an account.
  • • If you subscribe to OnlyBills Pro, Apple handles the purchase. The app checks with Apple whether your subscription is active. We never see your name, Apple ID or payment details.
  • • Anonymous usage analytics are sent to Google Firebase Analytics: which screens you open, which onboarding steps you complete, whether a scan succeeded, and subscription events such as subscribing to Pro. These are tied to a random app-install identifier, never to your name, email, or contacts, and no advertising identifier (IDFA) is collected. Receipt contents, item names, guest names, and payment handles are never included.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to make the app work:

  • • Receipt images are sent to Google's Gemini API to read the items and prices, then returned to your device
  • • During Live Split, the receipt items, the names guests enter, and their claims are stored in Google Firebase so everyone at the table can split the bill together in real time
  • • Payment details on a live table are used only to let guests pay you directly. OnlyBills never handles the money.
  • • Feedback notes are used only to improve OnlyBills and to reply to you
  • • Contact information is only used on your device to display names and photos when splitting bills
  • • Your receipt history and settings are stored locally on your device using Apple's secure data storage
  • • Anonymous usage analytics tell us which parts of the app people actually reach, for example how many people finish onboarding or whether receipt scanning is failing, so we can fix what isn't working

We do not:

  • • Collect personal information for advertising or profiling
  • • Collect an advertising identifier (IDFA) or track you across other apps and websites
  • • Include receipt contents, guest names, or payment handles in analytics
  • • Share your data with advertisers
  • • Sell your information to third parties

Data Storage

Most of your data stays on your device, stored using Apple's secure frameworks:

  • • Receipt history
  • • Contact selections
  • • Split calculations
  • • Payment handles and user preferences
  • • Your receipt scan count for the fair-use limit

Some data is stored off-device only when a feature needs it:

  • • Receipt images are transmitted to Google's Gemini API for scanning and are not retained by us
  • • When you start a Live Split, that bill's items, guest names, and claims are stored in Google Firebase, together with the payment details you chose to share, for the life of the shared session so guests can join and update it. You can end and remove a live table from the app.
  • • Anonymous usage analytics events are stored by Google Firebase Analytics under a random app-install identifier. Deleting the app resets that identifier.
  • • Feedback notes are stored in Google Firebase. Email us and we will delete yours.
  • • Cloudflare keeps a bill link's venue name and photo for up to 5 minutes so previews load quickly. It does not store the bill's items, names or payment details.

Third-Party Services

OnlyBills uses Google cloud services to power four things, Cloudflare to serve our website, and Apple for subscriptions:

Google Gemini API (receipt scanning): receipt images are transmitted over an encrypted connection, analysed, and structured item data is returned to your device.

Google Firebase (Live Split): when you share a bill, its items, the display names guests enter, and their claims are stored so everyone can split it in real time. Shared receipt images may be stored in Firebase Storage for the session. Feedback notes you send are stored here too.

Google Places (venue details): the restaurant name and address from your receipt are sent to find the venue's name and photo. No personal information is sent.

Google Firebase Analytics (product improvement): anonymous, aggregated usage events keyed to a random app-install identifier. No advertising identifier is collected and no receipt or contact data is included.

Cloudflare (website and bill link previews): requests to onlybills.app, including bill links, pass through Cloudflare. It processes them, including your IP address and browser details, only to deliver the site. For a bill link it reads the venue name and photo to build the link preview.

Apple (OnlyBills Pro): the optional subscription is bought, renewed and cancelled through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details.

All transfers use encrypted (HTTPS/TLS) connections. You can review these services' privacy policies:

  • • Google Gemini: https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms
  • • Google Firebase / Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • • Cloudflare: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/
  • • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Permissions

OnlyBills requests the following permissions:

Camera Access: Required to scan receipts. You can deny this and manually enter receipts instead.

Photo Library Access: Optional, allows you to import existing receipt images.

Contacts Access: Optional, makes it easier to select friends when splitting bills. You can use the app without granting contacts access.

Data Security

We take data security seriously:

  • • All local data is encrypted using Apple's secure data storage APIs
  • • Network transmissions use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS)
  • • We follow Apple's security guidelines and best practices
  • • No data is accessible to us or any third parties without your explicit action

Children's Privacy

OnlyBills is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • • You can delete all app data at any time through iOS Settings
  • • You can revoke permissions at any time through iOS Settings
  • • You can end a Live Split from the app, which removes that shared table
  • • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device and resets your anonymous analytics identifier
  • • You can email osullivan7791@gmail.com to request deletion of any data held for a Live Split session
