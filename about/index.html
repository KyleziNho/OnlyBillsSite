Hi, I’m Kyle.

I’m a computer science student at the University of Bath, and I built OnlyBills on my own.

It started the way it does for most people: a long table, one receipt, and someone doing sums on their phone while everyone else puts their coats on. The people who’d had a salad and a lemonade paid the same as the people who’d had three cocktails, and nobody wanted to be the one to say anything.

So I made the thing I wanted at the end of those dinners. You scan the receipt, your friends tick what they had on their own phones, and everyone pays you back the right amount. Shared dishes and service charge are handled properly, and the pennies add up.

What I care about

  • Your friends shouldn’t need an app. Only the person who paid needs OnlyBills. Everyone else joins from a link or QR code.
  • Exact money. Every share is worked out to the penny, so the total always matches the receipt.
  • Your money goes straight to you. Friends pay you through your own bank or PayPal. OnlyBills never holds it.
  • Privacy. Your receipt history stays on your phone. The details are in the privacy policy.
