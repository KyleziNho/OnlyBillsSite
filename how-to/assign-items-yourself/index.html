How to assign items yourself

No signal, or friends who won't get their phones out? Pick how many people, tap a face, tick their items, share the rest, and OnlyBills works out every share.

Kyle, founder of OnlyBills21 October 2026 · 3 min read

To assign items yourself, choose I'll assign it after checking the receipt. Set how many people there are, name them, then tap each person's face and tick what they had. Once every item has an owner, tap Review summary to see exactly what everyone owes.

This is the one to use when there's no signal (it works offline), or when it's quicker for you to do it than to get everyone to scan a QR code.

1. Choose "I'll assign it"

On the check page, tap Continue, then I'll assign it ("You pick who had what").

2. Say how many people

"How many people?" counts you too. Use the plus and minus buttons or the slider (anything from 2 to 25), then tap Continue. You can add or remove people later.

3. Name everyone

On "Who's splitting?", type each name or pick people from your contacts. Tap a face to choose a Memoji for someone. If you've split with the same group before, it appears under "Recent group" and you can tap Use to fill everyone in at once. Tap Start splitting.

4. Tap a face, then tick their items

The Split items screen has a dock of faces along the bottom. Tap a person's face, then tap each item they had. A label above the list reminds you who you're assigning to, for example "Assigning to Sam".

The bar at the top keeps score: "£34.90 of £37.40 assigned", plus how many items are left. Tap the items-left button to jump to the next item nobody has.

Tapped the wrong thing? Tap it again to take it off.

5. Share what was shared

For a dish that several people shared, select each of them in turn and tick the same item. It's split equally between everyone who has it, and the row says "Split 3 ways" (or however many).

For something the whole table shared, tap Everyone at the left of the dock, then tap the item. It's split between everyone.

Lines with more than one

If a line is for more than one of something (shown as ×2, ×3 and so on), tapping it opens "Split this item" with two options:

  • Who ordered what: set how many each person had, for example one beer for Sam and two for Priya.
  • Share equally: split the whole line evenly between the people you pick, for example two platters shared by five.

Tap Done to save.

6. Review the summary

When every item has someone, the bar says "All assigned" and Review summary appears in the dock.

The summary lists "Who owes what", with each person's total and their share of the bill. Service charge, tips and discounts are already included, spread in proportion to what each person had.

Tap a person to see what they had and a Request button for their amount. Tap the circle on the right of their row to mark them as paid. How to get paid back covers requests in detail.

Need to change something? Edit at the top right (or Edit items & charges further down) lets you fix items, prices and charges, and the shares update.

When you're done, tap Done. If anyone isn't ticked off yet, OnlyBills asks "Has everyone paid you?" so it knows who still owes you.

Questions people ask

Does assigning work without signal?

Yes. Assigning items yourself works offline. You only need a connection to read the receipt in the first place and to send payment requests.

Can I switch to a QR code halfway through?

Yes. The QR button at the top of the Split items screen shares a live table instead, and the people and items you've already assigned come with you.

What if I forget an item?

Review summary only appears once every item has someone. If you're stuck, tap the items-left button at the top to jump to whatever is still unassigned.

Do I need to include myself?

The count includes you by default, shown as "Me". If you're paying but didn't eat, just don't assign yourself any items.
