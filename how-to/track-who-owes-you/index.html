How to track who owes you

Owed shows everyone who still owes you across all your bills, with their faces. Settle up, record a part payment, send a nudge or add an IOU for anything else.

Kyle, founder of OnlyBills21 October 2026 · 3 min read

OnlyBills keeps a running list of who still owes you on the Owed page. Swipe across from the home screen, or tap Owed at the top. Everyone who owes you floats around your total, sized by how much they owe. Drag someone into the middle when they've paid, or tap them for the details.

How do people end up on Owed?

Only when you say so. When you finish a bill (tap Done on the summary, or Finish on a live table), OnlyBills asks "Has everyone paid you?" about anyone you haven't ticked off yet.

  • Yes, everyone's paid settles them all. This is the usual case.
  • Not yet asks "Who still owes you?". Pick the people who haven't paid. Everyone you don't pick is saved as paid.

Nobody is added as owing you by default. If you finished a bill and said everyone had paid, OnlyBills checks once more about a week later with a small card on the home screen: "Does anyone still owe you?". Tap Pick people if someone slipped through, or Everyone's paid to dismiss it.

1. Open Owed

The top of the page says how much you're owed and across how many friends. Faces float around the total, each with their amount underneath. Below that, the "Owes you" list shows each person, how many bills it's from and how long it's been.

The same person across different bills is counted once. If OnlyBills thinks two names might be the same person, it asks "Same person?" and you can tap Merge or Not the same.

2. Settle up when they pay

Three ways to mark someone as paid:

  • Drag their face into the middle. Press and hold a face, then drop it on the total.
  • Swipe left on their row in the "Owes you" list and tap Settle.
  • Tap them to open their bills, then tap Settle all.

A message at the bottom confirms who you settled, with Undo if you got the wrong person.

3. Record a part payment

Paid you some of it? Tap the person, then They paid some. OnlyBills asks how much they paid, with quick options for Half and All of it, and takes it off their oldest bills first.

4. Send a nudge

Tap the person and tap Nudge, or swipe left on their row. OnlyBills writes a friendly reminder with the total across every bill they owe for, and your payment link with the amount filled in where your bank allows it. It opens in Messages so you choose who to send it to, and you can edit it first. For example:

Hi Sam, quick reminder: you owe me £22.23 from Dishoom and Padella.

followed by your payment link.

Nudges are only sent when you tap the button. OnlyBills never messages anyone on its own.

5. Add an IOU

Lent someone a tenner for a taxi? Tap the + button at the top of Owed to add an IOU. Type a name and amount, and add a note if you like ("What for? (optional)"). You can also record money you owe someone else. That appears under "You owe".

Questions people ask

Does Owed track people automatically?

No. People only appear when you mark them as still owing at the end of a bill, or when you add an IOU yourself. OnlyBills doesn't connect to your bank, so you tick people off when the money arrives.

Can I see which bills someone owes for?

Yes. Tap their face or their row. You'll see each bill with the venue, date and their share.

What if I settle someone by mistake?

Tap Undo on the message that appears straight after. You can also add the amount back with Add an IOU.

Can I still use Owed without a subscription?

Yes. Owed, History and Settings stay open after your free split, even without a subscription. Only starting a new split needs one.
