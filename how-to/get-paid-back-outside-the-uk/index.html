How to get paid back with OnlyBills outside the UK

Choose your country and OnlyBills suggests how locals get paid: Bizum, MB WAY, Swish, Wero, IBAN and more. Here's how to set it up and what friends see.

Kyle, founder of OnlyBills14 November 2026 · 3 min read

To get paid back outside the UK, open Getting paid, check the country pill shows where you are, and pick the way you use most from the suggestions, for example Bizum in Spain or Swish in Sweden. Add the number, IBAN or link, then add a second option if you like. Friends see their exact share next to every option.

OnlyBills never holds the money. It shows friends how to pay you, and they pay from their own bank or app.

1. Check your country

OnlyBills asks during setup, and you can find it any time in Settings, under Getting paid. The first screen asks "How do friends pay you?" and shows up to three ways people commonly get paid in your country, plus More options.

The pill under the tiles shows the country OnlyBills picked. If it's wrong, or you live in one country and bank in another, tap Change. Choose Somewhere else if your country isn't listed: you'll get Revolut, PayPal and bank details. Changing country never removes what you've saved.

Some examples of what's suggested:

Country Suggested
United States Venmo, Cash App, Zelle
Canada Interac e-Transfer, PayPal
Spain Bizum, Revolut, PayPal
Portugal MB WAY, Revolut, PayPal
France Revolut, Wero, PayPal
Germany PayPal, Revolut, IBAN
Netherlands Tikkie or payment request link, Revolut, PayPal
Sweden Swish, Revolut, PayPal
Brazil Pix, PayPal

2. Add your details

Pick the one you use most and tap Continue. What you enter depends on the method:

  • Phone number apps (Bizum, MB WAY, Vipps, MobilePay, TWINT, BLIK, Wero, IRIS): your mobile number. It must be from that app's country, so a Bizum number starts +34.
  • IBAN: your IBAN and the name on the account. Use the exact name your bank has, because the payer's bank checks it.
  • A payment link (Tikkie, bunq, Revolut, PayPal and a few others): paste your link and tap Test link to check it opens. OnlyBills only accepts links it knows. If yours isn't one, it suggests adding your IBAN instead.

Under every number, email, IBAN or key, OnlyBills reminds you: "Everyone at your table will see it." If you'd rather not share it, use a link method such as Revolut or PayPal instead.

3. Add another way to get paid

Friends who can't use your first choice need another option. A visitor in Spain can't use Bizum, for example, but can pay a Revolut link by card or Apple Pay. In Getting paid, tap Add another way to get paid. The first method in your list is the one friends see first. What friends see shows a preview.

What friends see

Friends see their exact share at the top and your first choice underneath, with the rest under Other ways to pay. How each option works depends on the method:

Kind What the friend does
Copy and pay (Bizum, MB WAY, Zelle, Interac, Vipps, IBAN and others) Copies your number or IBAN, then the amount, into their own bank or payment app
Link (Revolut, PayPal, Venmo, a pasted Tikkie) Taps to open it. If the amount isn't filled in, they're told to enter it, for example "Enter 22,22 € when it opens"
Pix Copies a Pix Copia e Cola code with their amount already inside

If a method can't take the bill's currency, it moves below the others with the reason, for example "Bizum only takes euros." On a bill abroad you can also ask friends to pay you in your own currency: see how to get paid back in your own currency.

When a friend has paid, they tap I've paid and they're ticked off on your phone. OnlyBills doesn't see bank payments, so check your account before you chase anyone.

Questions people ask

Which countries does OnlyBills suggest payment methods for?

The UK, the US, Canada, most of the EU, Norway, Switzerland, Brazil, Argentina, Mexico, Colombia, Peru and Chile have their own suggestions. Anywhere else, choose Somewhere else for Revolut, PayPal and bank details.

Does changing country delete what I've saved?

No. Changing country only changes what's suggested. Everything you've saved stays.

Why does a friend see a method lower down with a note?

Some methods only take one currency, for example Bizum only takes euros. On a bill in another currency, OnlyBills moves that method below the others and says why.
