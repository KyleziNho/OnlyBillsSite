How to get paid back with OnlyBills

Add your Monzo, Starling, Revolut or PayPal link or bank details once. Then friends pay their exact share from a link, and you tick them off as the money lands.

Kyle, founder of OnlyBills21 October 2026 · 4 min read

To get paid back, add a way for friends to pay you: a Monzo, Starling, Revolut or PayPal link, or your bank details. You do it once. After that, friends on a live table see a pay button with their exact share, and on a bill you assigned yourself you can send each person a request with a link to pay.

The money goes straight from your friends to you. OnlyBills never holds it.

1. Choose how you get paid

OnlyBills asks during setup, and again the first time you share a table or send a request if you skipped it. You can also find it any time in Settings, under Getting paid. Tap your photo at the top left of the home screen to open Settings.

The first screen asks "Do you use any of these?" with Monzo, Starling, Revolut and PayPal.

Pick one and tap Continue. If you bank somewhere else, tap None of these. Any bank works for bank transfers. These four are suggested because they give you a payment link that friends can tap.

2. Add your link

OnlyBills shows two short steps for your app and an Open button (for example Open Monzo) that takes you there to copy your link:

  • Monzo: tap Payments, then Request and Share a link. Tap Copy.
  • Starling: tap Payments, then Request money, and copy your Settle Up link. Switch on Settle Up when Starling asks, or the link won't work for friends.
  • Revolut: tap your profile picture at the top left and copy your @Revtag or revolut.me link. Keep your Revtag discoverable, or some friends won't be able to pay you.
  • PayPal: tap your profile, then PayPal.me, and copy your paypal.me link.

Come back to OnlyBills and paste it, or type your username. Test link opens it so you can check it's yours.

3. Add your bank details

Next, OnlyBills asks for the name on your account, sort code and account number. If you set up an app, this is a backup for friends who'd rather transfer, and you can tap Skip for now. If you chose None of these, bank transfer is how friends pay you.

Check the numbers carefully. Transfers to the wrong account are hard to undo.

What your friends need

Your friends don't need the same bank as you. Here's what each option takes:

How you get paid What friends can pay with
Monzo Card, Apple Pay or bank transfer. No Monzo account needed
Starling Card or Apple Pay. No Starling account needed
Revolut Card or Apple Pay. No Revolut account needed
PayPal PayPal account may be needed
Bank transfer From any banking app

Monzo and PayPal links fill in the amount for your friend. With Starling and Revolut, they type the amount when the link opens.

4. Request payment

On a live table, friends pay from the table itself, so there's nothing to send. On a bill you assigned yourself, open the summary and tap a person. Their items appear with a Request button showing their amount, for example Request £13.58.

The Request payment sheet shows how much, and lets you choose how they pay you. Your default is ticked.

Tap Send request. If the person is in your contacts with a phone number, it opens a text to them. Otherwise you get the share sheet to send it however you like. The message looks like this:

Hi Sam, your share of Rajaji is £13.58.

followed by your payment link and a link where they can see their items.

5. Mark people as paid

When the money arrives, tick them off:

  • On the summary, tap the circle at the end of their row, or tap Mark as paid in the request sheet.
  • On a live table, friends who tap "I've paid" are ticked off for you. Tap someone's row in Settle up to tick them off yourself, for example if they paid in cash.

The bar at the top of the summary shows how much you've collected and how many people have paid.

Questions people ask

Can I change which method friends see first?

Yes. In Settings, under Getting paid, choose which method friends pay first with, or tap Add another way to get paid to add more.

Does OnlyBills take a fee?

No. Payments go from your friends to you through your bank or PayPal. OnlyBills doesn't process or hold the money.

Why isn't my bank in the list?

The four apps shown give you a payment link. Every other bank still works: tap None of these and add your account details, and friends pay you by bank transfer from any banking app.

What if someone still hasn't paid?

Say so when you finish the bill and they'll appear on your Owed page, where you can send a nudge. See How to track who owes you.
