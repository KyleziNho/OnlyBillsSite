How to share a live table so friends claim their own items

Show a QR code or send a link, and friends tick what they had on their own phone. Watch the claims come in, split what's left evenly, then finish the table.

Kyle, founder of OnlyBills21 October 2026 · 3 min read

A live table lets everyone claim their own items. After you've checked the receipt, choose Everyone taps their own. OnlyBills shows a QR code and a link. Friends scan it with their phone camera, add their name and tap what they had, with no app to download. You see every claim on your phone as it happens.

1. Go live

On the check page, tap Continue, then pick Everyone taps their own on the "How are you splitting?" sheet.

The first time, OnlyBills asks you to add a way to get paid (a Monzo, Starling, Revolut or PayPal link, or your bank details). That's what friends see when they tap to pay you. It takes about a minute, and the table opens straight after. How to get paid back walks through it.

2. Share the QR code or the link

The Live split screen shows a big QR code with the restaurant and total underneath.

There are three ways in:

  • Show the QR code. Friends point their phone camera at it. On iPhone it can open as an App Clip; on any phone it opens in the browser.
  • Copy link puts the link on your clipboard to paste into a group chat.
  • Send invite opens the share sheet with a ready-made message: "Tap what you had at Rajaji:" and the link.

Each person who joins appears under "at the table" with their face.

Someone without a phone?

Tap Add person, type their name, and you can claim items for them. A banner shows "Claiming for" and their name until you tap Done.

3. Watch the claims come in

Scroll down to see the bill. Each item shows who has claimed it, or "Up for grabs" if nobody has yet. The header keeps count, for example "2 up for grabs · £7.00".

You can claim your own items on this screen too: tap what you had. To change an item's name, price or quantity, hold it down and choose Edit item.

When two or three people tap the same dish, it's split between them. A line with more than one of something (say, garlic naan ×2) is shared by the people who tap it, and on your own screen (and for friends using the App Clip) there's a "How many did you have?" control too. Service charge and other extras are shared in proportion to what everyone ordered. The maths is in How shared items, service and tips are split.

4. Split what's left evenly

Nobody owning up to the poppadoms? Tap Split what's left evenly. Everything still up for grabs is shared between everyone at the table who hasn't paid yet. The line underneath says exactly who, for example "Between You, Sam, Priya & Maya".

5. Tick people off and finish

The Settle up section lists everyone with their total. When a friend pays through their link and taps that they've paid, they're ticked off on your phone. If someone pays you in cash, tap their row to tick them off yourself.

When everyone's in, tap Finish. If anyone hasn't been ticked off, OnlyBills asks "Has everyone paid you?". Tap Yes, everyone's paid, or Not yet and pick who still owes you. Those people go onto your Owed page.

Finish closes the table for everyone and saves it to History. Friends can still pay you from the link for 7 days.

Anything still up for grabs when you finish counts as yours, because you paid the restaurant. Use Split what's left evenly first if that isn't fair.

Questions people ask

Can I leave the table screen while friends are claiming?

Yes. The back button returns to the bill and the down arrow hides the table. It stays live either way, and you can get back to it from the home screen.

What if the signal is too weak to go live?

OnlyBills tells you it couldn't set the table and suggests assigning items yourself, which works offline. See How to assign items yourself.

Can I switch to assigning it myself halfway through?

Yes. Tap Split it myself instead under the share buttons. The table closes for everyone and what's already been tapped comes with you.

Can I run two tables at once?

No, one at a time. If you go live on a new bill while another table is still open, OnlyBills offers to finish the open one and save it to History first.
