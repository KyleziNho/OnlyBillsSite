To scan a receipt, tap **Scan receipt**, fit the whole receipt inside the frame, wait for "Receipt found", then tap the shutter button. OnlyBills reads every item, price, service charge and discount, then shows you a check page so you can confirm it adds up before anyone splits anything.

Most bad scans come down to light, creases or a cut-off bottom. Here's how to avoid them.

## 1. Set the receipt up

- **Lay it flat.** Put it on the table and smooth out any folds. A curled receipt bends the text and the numbers are what matter most.
- **Find some light.** Restaurants are dim. If the receipt looks grey on screen, tap the torch button in the camera to turn the torch on.
- **Keep the whole receipt in view.** The total and any service charge are usually at the bottom, and OnlyBills uses them to check its reading. If the camera says "Fit the whole receipt in view", move back a little.
- **Avoid shadows and glare.** Hold the phone straight above the receipt rather than at an angle.

## 2. Take the photo

When the pill says "Receipt found", tap the round shutter button. You'll see "Reading receipt…" while the items come in line by line.

Already took a photo? Tap the photo button on the home screen, or the Photos button inside the camera, and pick it from your library.

### Long receipts

OnlyBills reads long receipts in one photo. Step back until the top line and the total both fit in the frame, keep the receipt flat, and make sure the text is still sharp. If the paper is too long to fit and stay legible, scan it anyway and add any missing lines by hand on the next screen (see step 4).

## 3. Check it adds up

The check page lists every item with its price. Above the **Continue** button, a pill compares what OnlyBills read with the total printed on the receipt:

- **Receipt adds up**: the items and charges match the printed total, so it's very likely right.
- **Check this**: there's a difference, shown in pounds. A missed or misread item is the usual cause.
- **No total to check**: OnlyBills couldn't find a printed total, so give the items a quick look yourself.

:::shot host-03-review | The check page for Rajaji. "Fix anything we misread" sits above the items, and the pill says the receipt adds up.

Tap the pill to see the figures side by side: what it read, what the receipt says, and the difference.

If you tap **Continue** while it doesn't add up, OnlyBills tells you how far over or under the printed total it is. You can tap **Fix** to go back, or **Continue anyway**. Everyone splits the items as listed, so it's worth fixing first.

## 4. Fix a misread line

Tap any item (each row has a small pencil). It opens so you can change:

- the name
- the price, as printed on the receipt
- the quantity, with the **Qty** stepper
- a discount on that item

Use the bin button to delete a line, and **Add item** at the bottom of the list for anything the scan missed. Service charge, tips and discounts sit in their own section under the items, where you can **Add charge or tip** or **Add discount**, either as an amount or a percentage.

## 5. No receipt? Type it in

On the home screen, tap the pencil button next to **Scan receipt** to open **Manual entry**. Add a store name, then either add each item with **Add item**, or skip the items and use **Or enter the total**. You can add charges and discounts here too. Tap **Next** and you land on the same check page as a scan.

If a scan fails, OnlyBills offers **Enter manually** as well, so you're never stuck.

## FAQ

### What if I have no signal when I scan?
OnlyBills needs a connection to read the receipt. If you're offline it offers **Save for later**, keeps the photo, and reads it when you're back online. You can also enter the bill by hand straight away.

### Does it pick up service charge and discounts?
Yes. Printed service charges, tips and discounts are read as separate lines, not as items, so they can be shared fairly. Check them in the charges section under the items.

### What happens to my receipt photo?
The photo is sent over an encrypted connection to Google's AI to read the items and prices, and OnlyBills doesn't keep it. If you share a live table, the bill and receipt photo are stored so everyone at the table can see them. OnlyBills never sells your data.

### Can I scan a receipt in another language?
Settings has a **Translate to English** option that auto-translates receipt items written in other languages.
