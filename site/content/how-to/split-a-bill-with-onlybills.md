Splitting a bill with OnlyBills takes four steps: scan the receipt, check it adds up, choose how to split it, then collect what you're owed. Only you need the app. Your friends can claim their items from a link or QR code in their phone's browser.

This page is the quick version. Each step links to a longer tutorial if you want the detail.

:::video live-table | Friends scan the QR code and join your table.

## 1. Scan the receipt

Open OnlyBills and tap **Scan receipt**. Hold your phone over the receipt until the pill at the top says "Receipt found", then tap the shutter button. OnlyBills reads every item, price, service charge and discount.

:::shot host-01-home | The home screen. Scan receipt is the big button; the pencil is for typing a bill in, and the photo button imports a picture you already took.

No receipt to hand, or the paper is too crumpled? The pencil button next to **Scan receipt** opens **Manual entry**, where you type the items or just the total. The photo button lets you pick a receipt photo from your library.

More tips: [How to scan a receipt](/how-to/scan-a-receipt/).

## 2. Check it adds up

The next screen lists everything OnlyBills read. A pill above **Continue** tells you whether the items and charges match the total printed on the receipt. "Receipt adds up" means they do.

:::shot host-03-review | The check page for a bill at Rajaji. Five items, and a green pill saying the receipt adds up.

If something's wrong, tap the line (each has a small pencil) to fix its name, price or quantity, or tap **Add item** for anything that was missed. When you're happy, tap **Continue**.

## 3. Choose how to split it

OnlyBills asks "How are you splitting?" and gives you two ways.

:::shot host-09-how-splitting | Two choices: Everyone taps their own (share a QR code) or I'll assign it (you pick who had what).

- **Everyone taps their own**: you get a QR code and a link. Friends open it on their phone, add their name and tick what they had. You watch the claims come in. See [How to share a live table](/how-to/share-a-live-table/).
- **I'll assign it**: you choose how many people there are, then tap each person's face and tick their items. Handy when the signal is poor, because it works offline. See [How to assign items yourself](/how-to/assign-items-yourself/).

If you paid and nobody needs to pay you back, tap **Just me paying? Save to History** instead.

Shared dishes are split between the people who shared them. Service charge, tips and discounts are spread in proportion to what each person had, and every share is worked out to the penny. The detail is in [How shared items, service and tips are split](/how-to/shared-items-service-and-tips/).

## 4. Get paid back

On a live table, friends pay you from their own phone with your Monzo, Starling, Revolut or PayPal link, or by bank transfer. If you assigned it yourself, the summary shows who owes what. Tap a person to see their items and a **Request** button, which sends them a message with their exact amount and your payment link.

:::shot host-06-summary | The summary for Rajaji: who owes what, and how much of it has been collected so far.

Tick people off as they pay. When you tap **Done** (or **Finish** on a live table), OnlyBills asks "Has everyone paid you?". Anyone you say still owes you shows up on the **Owed** page until they settle. See [How to get paid back](/how-to/get-paid-back/) and [How to track who owes you](/how-to/track-who-owes-you/).

## FAQ

### Do my friends need the app?
No. If you share a live table, friends open it in their browser or as an App Clip on iPhone. If you assign items yourself, they don't need to do anything until your payment request arrives.

### What does OnlyBills cost?
Your first split is free. After that it's £0.99 a month or £5.99 a year, with a free trial. The paywall shows the trial length and price before you start, and you can cancel any time in your iPhone's Settings.

### Can I practise before a real bill?
Yes. During setup you can try a practice scan on a pretend receipt, and you can replay it any time from Settings under Walkthrough. Nothing is saved, and it doesn't use your free split.

### Can I change the bill after I've split it?
Yes. On the summary, tap **Edit** or **Edit items & charges** to change items, prices and charges. The shares update to match.
