If a friend has sent you an OnlyBills link or shown you a QR code, you don't need to download anything. Open it, type your name, tap the things you had, and your share adds up as you go. Then tap the pay button to pay whoever paid the bill, straight to them.

It works in your phone's browser, on iPhone or Android. On an iPhone it may open as an [App Clip](/blog/what-is-an-app-clip/), which is a small piece of the app that runs without installing it.

## 1. Open the table

Scan the QR code with your phone's camera, or tap the link in your messages. You'll see the restaurant's name, who's hosting, and how many people are at the table.

## 2. Add your name

Under "Who are you?", type your name and tap **Join the table** (it changes to "Join as" and your name once you start typing). Your name is what everyone else at the table sees.

:::shot guest-01-join | Joining a table at Saffron House. Type your name, or tap That's me if you're already listed.

Already joined on another phone? Look under "Already at the table" and tap **That's me** next to your name to pick up where you left off.

## 3. Tap what you had

Scroll to "The bill" and tap each thing you ordered. A tick appears and it's added to your share. Tap it again to take it off. Items nobody has claimed yet say "Up for grabs".

:::shot guest-02-claimed | Alex has ticked a Lamb Raan Bun and shares the garlic naan with two others. Their share is £22.22.

### Shared dishes

If you shared something, tap it even if someone else already has. It's then split between everyone who tapped it, and the row shows "Split 3 ways" (or however many) with your part underneath, for example "You £1.60".

### More than one of something

A line like "Garlic Naan ×2" is shared between everyone who taps it, the same as a shared dish. If the table opened as an App Clip on your iPhone, it also asks "How many did you have?" once you've claimed it, so you can set your number with the plus and minus buttons.

### Something wrong on the bill?

Tap **Something wrong? Edit items** to fix a name, price or quantity, or **Add something missing**. Everyone at the table sees the change.

## 4. Check your share

The card at the top shows your total, how many items you have, and how much of it is service and other extras. Tap **Show breakdown** to see each item and your part of the extras.

Service charge and extras are shared in proportion to what you ordered, so a side salad doesn't pay the same service as a steak.

:::shot guest-05-live | Your share stays up to date as others claim. A pill at the top shows when someone else has paid.

## 5. Pay the host

Tap the pay button at the bottom (it says "Pay" and the host's name, with your amount). A sheet opens with the host's main way to get paid as a big button, and any others under "Other ways to pay".

:::shot guest-03-pay-sheet | Pay Kyle £22.22 via Monzo, with Revolut, PayPal and bank transfer as other ways to pay.

What you'll need depends on how the host gets paid:

| Payment option | What you can pay with |
|---|---|
| Monzo | Card, Apple Pay or bank transfer. No Monzo account needed |
| Starling | Card or Apple Pay. No Starling account needed |
| Revolut | Card or Apple Pay. No Revolut account needed |
| PayPal | PayPal account may be needed |
| Bank transfer | From any banking app |

With Monzo and PayPal links, your amount is filled in for you. With Starling and Revolut you type the amount when it opens (the sheet shows it). For a bank transfer, tap **Copy** next to the name, sort code and account number and paste them into your banking app.

The money goes straight to the host. OnlyBills never holds it.

## 6. Tell the host you've paid

Once you've paid, tap the "I've paid" button (it includes the host's name). It ticks you off on their phone and the button turns green.

:::shot guest-04-paid | Paid. The card confirms "You paid Kyle £22.22".

Tapped it by mistake? Open the sheet again and tap **Not paid yet? Undo**.

## FAQ

### Do I need to download OnlyBills?
No. The table works in any modern phone browser, on iPhone or Android. Only the person hosting the bill needs the app.

### What if my share changes after I've paid?
If someone edits the bill and your share goes up, the page tells you by how much and the button changes to **Pay the extra**. If it goes down, it shows how much you've overpaid so you can sort it out with the host.

### What happens when the host finishes the table?
Claims lock and the totals are final. You can still open the link, pay, and mark yourself as paid for 7 days afterwards.

### Can I join on a new phone?
Yes. Open the same link and tap **That's me** next to your name under "Already at the table".
