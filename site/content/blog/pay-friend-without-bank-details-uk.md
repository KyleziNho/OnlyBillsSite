The easiest way is to ask your friend for their payment link. A Monzo, Revolut, Starling or PayPal link lets you pay from your phone without typing in a sort code, and you usually don't need an account with their bank. If they don't have a link, you can send money as a link from your own bank, and they claim it with their own details.

Here's what each option needs from you, what it costs, how quickly it lands and where the limits are. We checked every bank and app below against its own help pages in November 2026. Apps change, so check the screen in front of you too.

## What's the quickest option?

Ask for their link. Most people who bank with Monzo, Revolut or Starling have one, and many high street banks now have a request link too. A link carries their name, so you can see who you're paying before you pay.

You can copy this:

> Hey, what do I owe you for Saturday? Send me your Monzo or PayPal link (or whatever you use) and I'll pay you now.

## What do you need to pay each kind of link?

| Their link | You pay with | Limits we could verify |
|---|---|---|
| Monzo | Card, Apple Pay or bank transfer | Not published |
| Revolut | Card or Apple Pay | £250 a week by card |
| Starling | Card or Apple Pay | £250 a day, £1 minimum |
| PayPal | PayPal | Check in PayPal |
| NatWest | Your own banking app, via Payit | £500 per request |
| Lloyds | Your own bank, picked on a web page | £150 per request |

- **Account needed?** Not for Monzo, Revolut or Starling links: you don't need an account with their bank. PayPal may need a PayPal account. NatWest's link needs online or mobile banking with a major UK bank, and Lloyds' lets you pick your own UK bank on its page.
- **Cards:** Monzo says credit and prepaid cards can't be used to pay its links.
- **The fine print:** Revolut's £250 a week cap is on card payments into the link, for the person receiving. Starling's £250 a day is what its user can receive by UK debit card. NatWest's single-use links expire after 5 days. Lloyds allows up to £500 a day received.

All limits are as of November 2026, from each bank's help pages (linked in the sources).

**Fees:** Starling and Monzo don't list a fee for paying their links on the pages we checked, and NatWest says its request service is free. PayPal charges no fee for a UK friends and family payment from your PayPal balance or bank account, when there's no currency conversion.

**Speed:** a card or Apple Pay payment shows on the page as soon as it goes through. A bank transfer goes by Faster Payments, which Pay.UK says is usually available almost immediately but can sometimes take up to two hours ([how long a bank transfer takes](/blog/how-long-does-bank-transfer-take-uk/)).

### A worked example

Jas paid £96.00 for four of you at a curry house. Your share is £24.00. Jas sends a Starling Settle Up link. You open it, type £24.00, pay with Apple Pay, and you're done without ever seeing Jas's sort code. If Jas had sent a Revolut link instead, the same thing works by card or Apple Pay. The £250 weekly card cap only matters if lots of people pay Jas through the link that week.

## What if they don't have a payment link?

Send the money as a link from your side. You pay into a link, and your friend claims it with their own details.

- **Monzo:** in Payments, tap Pay someone, then Pay someone new, then **Pay anyone using a link**. The money stays in your account until they claim it, and you can cancel while it's unclaimed. Monzo's own advice: don't add it to group messages, because whoever claims it gets it.
- **Revolut:** Revolut's help pages describe sending money with a link. Your friend enters their details to claim, and if it isn't claimed within 7 days the money goes back to you.
- **NatWest:** Pay Your Contacts sends money to a mobile number, up to £250 per working day. Your friend doesn't need to bank with NatWest. If they aren't registered, NatWest sends them a secure link to claim it.

Other banks have their own versions, so look in your app under payments for "pay by link" or "pay a contact".

:::note
Paym, the old pay-by-mobile-number service run across UK banks, closed on 7 March 2023, according to Pay.UK. If an old guide tells you to use it, that's why it isn't there.
:::

## Can you pay with PayPal using just an email or phone number?

Yes, if they use PayPal. You send it to their email address or mobile number from your PayPal account. They'll need a PayPal account to receive it, so this works best with friends who already have one. PayPal's UK fees page lists no fee for a UK friends and family payment from your PayPal balance or bank account (as of November 2026).

## Is cash still an option?

Of course. It needs nothing from either of you, and it's done on the spot. The downside is that almost nobody carries it now, so it only works if you've got the right notes on you. We wrote about that shift in [nobody carries cash any more](/blog/nobody-carries-cash/).

## How do you check a link before you pay?

Three quick checks:

1. **It came from your friend**, in a chat you already have with them. Not from a new number.
2. **The name on the payment page is theirs.** Monzo, Revolut, Starling and PayPal pages show who you're paying.
3. **The address is the real one:** monzo.me, revolut.me, settleup.starlingbank.com or paypal.me.

If any of those look off, message your friend directly before you pay. There's more on this in [is it safe to share your bank details](/blog/is-it-safe-to-share-bank-details/).

## FAQ

### Can I pay someone in the UK with just their phone number?
Sometimes. NatWest's Pay Your Contacts sends up to £250 per working day to a mobile number, and the person doesn't need to bank with NatWest. PayPal works with a phone number if they have PayPal. Paym, the old cross-bank service, closed in March 2023.

### Do I need a Monzo account to pay a monzo.me link?
No. Anyone can pay a Monzo link, though Monzo says you can't use a credit card or a prepaid card to pay through it.

### Is it free to pay a friend back by link?
Usually, for the person paying. PayPal lists no fee for UK friends and family payments from your balance or bank account, and NatWest says its request service is free. Check the payment page before you confirm.

### What's the most I can pay through a payment link?
It depends on the link. Starling says its users can receive up to £250 a day by UK debit card, and Revolut says card payments into a link are typically capped at £250 a week. For bigger amounts, a bank transfer is simpler.
