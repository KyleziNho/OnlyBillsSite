Cash App doesn't have a split button. To split a bill, work out each person's share and send each friend a **request**, either to their $cashtag or as a link you text them. For a bigger group or a cost you're collecting ahead of time, start a **Pool**. Friends without Cash App can pay a request link or a Pool with Apple Pay or Google Pay.

That last part is Cash App's real advantage for splitting: unlike Venmo, the friend who isn't on it doesn't have to sign up. We checked the steps against Cash App's own pages and terms on 14 November 2026.

## Option 1: request from each friend

Best for a dinner where everyone owes a different amount.

1. Open Cash App and go to the payment screen (the **$** tab).
2. Enter the friend's share (minimum $1) and tap **Request**.
3. Add a note, such as "Thursday dinner, incl. tax and tip".
4. Either search for them by name, $cashtag, phone number or email, or choose to **send a link**.
5. Tap **Request**.

Repeat for each person. It's one request per friend, which is a little slower than a split button, but it means each person gets their own exact number.

To work out the numbers, use [the tax and tip formula](/blog/split-check-tax-and-tip/): each person's items ÷ subtotal × total.

## Option 2: Pools, for groups and costs you collect up front

A Pool is a shared pot. You name it, set a goal amount and invite people, and Cash App tracks who's paid.

- Start it from the payment screen, give it a name and a goal.
- Invite friends in Cash App by $cashtag, or share the Pool link by text or anywhere else.
- When you're done, close the Pool and move the money to your Cash balance. You can close it before it hits the goal, but nobody can add money after that.

There's no deadline. Pools suit the things you collect for before you spend: a group gift, a vacation rental deposit, concert tickets. For a dinner where everyone ordered different amounts, individual requests are usually better, because a Pool shows one goal, not what each person owes.

Cash App first released Pools to a small group of users in 2025. If you don't see it, update the app or use requests.

## Friends without Cash App: payment links

This is the bit people don't expect. When you request money as a link, Cash App says someone without Cash App can pay you "through a payment link with Apple Pay or Google Pay".

Cash App's terms for paying without an account say:

- No Cash App account is needed to pay.
- They pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay, not a typed-in card. Prepaid and gift cards from other companies aren't allowed.
- They must live in the US and be 18 or older.
- It's free, except a credit card in Apple Pay or Google Pay, which costs 3%.
- Cash App can hold a payment for up to 15 days in some cases.

## $cashtag links vs payment links

There are two kinds of Cash App link, and they're easy to mix up:

| | $cashtag link (cash.app/$name) | Payment link (made with Request) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | Your public profile | One request for one amount |
| Where it's made | It's always there | In Cash App, each time you request |
| Amount | Your friend types it | Set by you |
| Friend without Cash App | Not something we've confirmed works | Can pay with Apple Pay or Google Pay |

If you're collecting from someone who doesn't use Cash App, send them a payment link, not your $cashtag.

## FAQ

### Can you split a payment on Cash App?
Not with a split button. Send each friend a request for their share, or start a Pool and share the link. Requests can go to people on Cash App or out as a link by text.

### Can someone pay me on Cash App without an account?
Yes, through a payment link. When you send a request as a link, a friend without Cash App can pay it with Apple Pay or Google Pay. They need to be in the US and 18 or older. Pools work the same way, except for New York residents, who need an account to contribute.

### Does Cash App charge to request money?
No. Requesting is free, and paying from a Cash balance or linked debit card is free. Paying with a credit card, including through Apple Pay or Google Pay, costs 3%.

### Is Cash App or Venmo better for splitting a bill?
Venmo has a built-in split and is rolling out receipt scanning, so it's easier when everyone uses Venmo. Cash App is better when some friends don't have it, because they can pay a payment link with Apple Pay or Google Pay without signing up.
