All three banks let you split a card payment after you've made it: open the transaction in the app, tap split, pick your friends and send requests. The big difference is who can pay you. Revolut's split bill only works with other Revolut customers, while Monzo and Starling can send a payment link to friends at any bank.

Here's exactly how each one works, where each one falls short, and what to do when your friends bank somewhere else. We checked every step against each bank's own help pages in October 2026. Menus do move, so if a button has a slightly different name in your app, that's why.

## At a glance

| | Monzo | Revolut | Starling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feature name | Monzo Split (Single Split and Running Splits) | Split bill, plus Group Bills | Split the Bill, using Settle Up links |
| Split a past card payment | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Uneven amounts | Yes, you can edit each amount | Yes, adjust each person's amount | Splits by number of people; send a custom Settle Up link for uneven amounts |
| Friends at other banks | Yes, with a request link | Not with Split bill (Revolut customers only); yes with Group Bills or a payment link | Yes, with a Settle Up link |
| How non-customers pay | Through Monzo's payment link | Card via a Revolut.me link | Secure web page, UK debit card |
| Cost | Free | Free | Free |

## How to split a bill with Monzo

Monzo calls this Monzo Split. A Single Split is for one payment, like a dinner bill.

1. Pay the bill on your Monzo card.
2. Open the payment in your transactions and tap **Split this bill**.
3. Choose friends from Nearby Friends or your Monzo contacts. For someone not on Monzo, tap **Next** and add them as a non-Monzo contact.
4. Split equally, or edit the amounts for each person.
5. Send. Friends on Monzo get a notification to accept. For everyone else, share the request link by WhatsApp, text or however you like.

Monzo also has **Running Splits** for ongoing costs like a trip or household bills: you add people and expenses as you go and settle up at the end. Monzo says friends can join as guests from a link, even if they don't live in the UK or bank with Monzo, and that Split is free for you and your friends.

**Limits to know:** you can only cancel a Single Split if the other person is a Monzo customer. A request sent to a non-Monzo friend can't be withdrawn.

## How to split a bill with Revolut

1. Pay on your Revolut card.
2. Tap the payment in your transactions list, then **Split bill**.
3. Choose the Revolut contacts you want to split with and tap **Continue**.
4. Split evenly, or adjust the amount each person owes.
5. Each person gets a request they can accept or decline. Once they accept, the money lands in your account.

Revolut says there's no charge for splitting bills. You can also auto-split recurring payments by choosing "This and future payments".

**The limit:** Revolut's help page is titled "How to split a bill with another Revolut customer", and that's the catch. Everyone you split with needs Revolut. For friends who don't, you have two options:

- **A payment link.** Request money with a Revolut.me link and the sender can pay by card or Revolut transfer. Revolut's help pages say card payments via links are capped, typically at £250 a week, and requests expire after 10 days.
- **Group Bills.** Revolut's shared expense tracker for trips and households. Its help page says you can invite people without a Revolut account and they can join without signing up. Note that Revolut's terms describe Group Bills as an expense tracker, not a payment service: it records who owes what, and you still need to settle up.

## How to split a bill with Starling

Starling's Split the Bill sits on top of its Settle Up payment links, so set that up first.

1. **Switch on Settle Up:** go to Payments, then Request money, and switch on your link.
2. Pay the bill on your Starling card.
3. On Home, tap the transaction and choose **Split the bill**.
4. Pick the number of people. Starling works out each share.
5. Send each person their Settle Up link.

If your friend banks with Starling, the link opens their app. If not, it opens a secure web page where they pay with a UK debit card (Starling's help pages also mention Apple Pay and Google Pay). Nobody has to swap sort codes or set up a new payee.

**Limits to know:** Starling's Settle Up page says you can receive up to £250 a day by UK debit card through Settle Up, with a £1 minimum. Split the Bill divides by headcount, so for uneven shares send each person a Settle Up link for their own amount.

## Where bank split features fall short

Bank split buttons are free and already on your phone, so they're great when the split is simple. They struggle in three situations.

**They split a total, not the receipt.** None of them reads the bill line by line. If one person had a £9 salad and another had steak and three cocktails, you're working out the amounts yourself before you type them in (the free [split the bill calculator](/tools/split-bill-calculator/) helps with that part).

**Some only work within the bank.** Revolut's Split bill needs everyone on Revolut. Even with Monzo and Starling, friends at other banks get a link rather than the full experience.

**Service charge is on you.** If the bill includes 12.5% service, you need to add it to each person's share in proportion, or someone ends up paying for everyone else's tip. [How to split service charge and tips fairly](/blog/split-service-charge-fairly/) shows the method.

### A worked example

Dinner for three comes to £90.00, including £10.00 service on £80.00 of food.

| Person | Food | Their service (12.5%) | Owes |
|---|---|---|---|
| You | £30.00 | £3.75 | £33.75 (you paid) |
| Sam | £18.00 | £2.25 | £20.25 |
| Priya | £32.00 | £4.00 | £36.00 |

In Monzo, you'd split the payment and edit the amounts to £20.25 and £36.00. In Revolut, the same, if Sam and Priya are on Revolut. In Starling, you'd send two Settle Up links for those amounts.

## How payment links work for friends at other banks

You don't need everyone on the same bank to get paid back. You have three options. The first two don't need your friend to open anything new, and the third only works smoothly if they already use PayPal.

- **Your bank's payment link:** Monzo's request link, a Revolut.me link or a Starling Settle Up link. Your friend taps it and pays on a web page, usually by card. Check each bank's limits (above) for bigger amounts.
- **A plain bank transfer:** send your name, sort code and account number. Your friend pays from any UK banking app by Faster Payments. No limits from a link, but they have to type your details in.
- **PayPal:** works if they already use it, though they may need a PayPal account.

## FAQ

### Can I split a bill on Monzo with someone who isn't on Monzo?
Yes. In a Single Split, tap Next, add them as someone not on Monzo, and share the request link. Monzo's help pages note you can't cancel a request sent to a non-Monzo customer.

### Does Revolut split bill work with non-Revolut users?
Not the Split bill button, which is for other Revolut customers. Use a Revolut.me payment link (card payments via links are typically capped at £250 a week) or invite them to a Group Bills group.

### Is there a limit on Starling Settle Up?
Starling's Settle Up page says you can receive up to £250 a day by UK debit card, with a £1 minimum per payment. Bank transfers to your sort code and account number aren't affected.

### Do bank split features charge fees?
Monzo says Split is free for you and your friends, Revolut says there's no charge for splitting bills, and Starling doesn't list a fee for Settle Up.
