All three apps can split a bill. In Swish, open **Tillsammans** and choose **Dela kostnad** to split a cost between up to 10 people. In Vipps, tap **Request** and choose **Split an amount**. In MobilePay, send a request to each person, or use its group expenses feature for an ongoing pot.

The catch is that all three need a local bank account, so a visiting friend usually can't pay. Here's how each app splits, how to handle uneven shares, and what to do about visitors.

## Swish (Sweden)

Swish is how Swedes pay each other. To split a bill:

1. Open Swish and tap **Tillsammans** in the menu.
2. Choose **Dela kostnad**. The first time, accept the terms.
3. Add up to 10 people, and remember to include yourself.
4. Enter the total. Swish works out each person's share and sends everyone a request.

Each friend pays their share from their own Swish. Dela kostnad is built for even splits. If people ordered different things, send each person a normal Swish request for their own amount instead.

Swish needs a Swedish bank account and Mobile BankID on the same phone, and BankID needs a Swedish personal identity number (personnummer).

## Vipps (Norway)

Vipps has two ways to ask several people for money. Tap **Request** on the home screen, then:

- **Request:** ask one or more people for the same amount.
- **Split an amount:** pick who's sharing first, enter the total, and Vipps divides it equally. You can then adjust each person's share, which makes it the best of the three for uneven splits.

For ongoing shared costs, like a holiday or a shared flat's shopping, Vipps groups let everyone add expenses and work out who owes whom.

## MobilePay (Denmark and Finland)

MobilePay lets you request money from contacts or phone numbers, and has a feature for shared group expenses, the successor to the old WeShare app, where costs can be split equally, by amount or by percentage. Vipps and MobilePay are run by one company, Vipps MobilePay, but they're still separate apps in each country, and MobilePay in Finland works in euros.

## Uneven shares: work them out first

None of these apps reads a receipt. For a dinner where people had different things, work out each share first, then request it.

**Example: four friends in Stockholm, bill 1,200 kr.**

| | Elin | Johan | Sara | Ali |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ordered | 340 kr | 380 kr | 260 kr | 220 kr |
| Share of 10% tip (in proportion) | 34 kr | 38 kr | 26 kr | 22 kr |
| **Pays** | **374 kr** | **418 kr** | **286 kr** | **242 kr** |

Elin paid the 1,320 kr total including the tip. With Dela kostnad, everyone would pay 330 kr, so Ali would overpay by 88 kr. Better to send three separate requests: 418 kr to Johan, 286 kr to Sara and 242 kr to Ali.

For more on tips in the Nordics (usually a round-up), see [tipping and service charge by country](/blog/tipping-and-service-charge-by-country/).

## What if a friend at the table doesn't have Swish or Vipps?

Visiting friends, and many expats in their first months, can't use these apps. They can pay you by:

- **Revolut,** if you have it. Friends without Revolut can pay a Revolut payment link by card or Apple Pay.
- **PayPal,** if you both have it.
- **Bank transfer.** In Finland that's an IBAN transfer in euros. In Sweden, Norway and Denmark, a transfer from abroad is an international payment in kronor, which can be slow and costly from a foreign bank.

If they're paying from another currency, agree the rate first. See [splitting a bill abroad](/blog/split-bill-abroad-currency/).

For an all-Swedish table splitting evenly, Swish's own Dela kostnad is enough. OnlyBills helps when people had different things, or when someone at the table can't use Swish.

## FAQ

### Can you split a bill in Swish?
Yes. In the Swish app, go to Tillsammans and choose Dela kostnad. You can split a cost between up to 10 people, including yourself, and everyone gets a request for their share.

### Can Swish split a bill unevenly?
Dela kostnad divides the cost for you. For different amounts, send each person a separate Swish request with their own share.

### How do you request money from several people in Vipps?
Tap Request on the home screen. Choose Request to ask several people for the same amount, or Split an amount to divide a total, then adjust each person's share if they owe different amounts.

### Can a tourist pay with Swish?
Usually not. Swish needs a Swedish bank account and Mobile BankID, which needs a Swedish personal identity number. Visitors can pay by Revolut, PayPal or bank transfer instead.
