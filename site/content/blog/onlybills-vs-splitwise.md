Use Splitwise when costs build up over time between the same people: housemates, a couple, a two week trip. For a single restaurant bill, both apps now let one person scan the receipt and everyone else claim their own items from a QR code, so the choice comes down to how you want to get paid back and how your group already works.

We make OnlyBills, so read this with that in mind. We've tried to be fair: every Splitwise feature and price below comes from Splitwise's own website, its UK App Store listing or launch coverage, checked in October 2026.

## What changed in October 2026

On 16 October 2026, Splitwise launched **Itemize** in the US and UK, according to The Paypers. One person photographs the receipt. Everyone else scans a QR code and claims what they had, without downloading the app or making an account. It's free for all Splitwise users. That closes most of the gap that used to exist between the two apps for restaurant bills, and it's a good feature. If your group already lives in Splitwise, start there.

## What Splitwise does well

Splitwise is very good at its main job. You add expenses as they happen and it keeps one running balance per group. Debt simplification then reduces a tangle of IOUs to the fewest possible payments: if Amy owes Ben £20, Ben owes Chloe £20 and Chloe owes Amy £5, it boils that down to one payment, Amy pays Chloe £15.

It handles recurring expenses (rent, broadband), lets you split by exact amounts, percentages or shares, and runs on iPhone, Android and the web. In the UK it has offered Pay by Bank through Tink since April 2024, so friends can pay you from their bank account inside Splitwise. For a shared house, nothing on this page beats it.

The free version caps how many expenses you can add each day. Splitwise Pro removes the cap and adds currency conversion, charts, search and no ads. The UK App Store lists Pro in-app purchases from £2.79 to £47.99 depending on the plan.

## What OnlyBills does differently

OnlyBills only does one thing: split a bill like the one in front of you at a restaurant. The host scans the receipt and it reads every line. You check the total adds up, then share a live table. Friends scan a QR code or open a link, pick their name and tick what they had. Everyone sees their share add up live, and who has claimed what.

:::shot guest-05-live | Each person sees their own share, with shared items split between the people who had them.

Where it differs from Splitwise Itemize:

**Getting paid back.** Each friend sees their exact amount and pays you with a link for the bank you already use. With Monzo that's card, Apple Pay or bank transfer, with no Monzo account needed. With Starling or Revolut it's card or Apple Pay, no account needed. There's PayPal (a PayPal account may be needed) and plain bank transfer from any banking app. Nobody has to join anything to pay. OnlyBills doesn't hold the money; it hands off to your bank or PayPal.

**UK bills.** It's built around how UK receipts look: a discretionary 12.5% service charge, a discount, a cover charge. They're spread across everyone in proportion to what they had, and the shares are exact to the penny and always add up to the bill.

**The friend who won't tap anything.** The host can assign items for people as well as letting them claim their own, so the bill still gets split when someone ignores the link.

**Owed.** It keeps track of who still owes you across bills, with their faces, so you can chase without digging through your banking app.

## A worked example: Friday dinner for five

The bill is £161.10: £143.20 of food and drink plus 12.5% service (£17.90). Three people shared a £27 bottle of wine.

| Person | Their items | Share of wine | Subtotal | With service |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amy | £24.00 | £9.00 | £33.00 | £37.13 |
| Ben | £19.50 | £9.00 | £28.50 | £32.06 |
| Chloe | £16.20 | £9.00 | £25.20 | £28.35 |
| Dev | £22.00 | none | £22.00 | £24.75 |
| Esme | £34.50 | none | £34.50 | £38.81 |
| **Total** | | | **£143.20** | **£161.10** |

An equal split would be £32.22 each. Dev, who didn't drink, would pay £7.47 more than his share.

Both apps get to these numbers in roughly the same way: scan, share a QR code, everyone claims. The difference is the last step. In Splitwise, the amounts land in Splitwise, where friends pay through the app or add them to a balance. In OnlyBills, Dev gets "£24.75" and your Monzo or bank link, and pays from whatever bank he uses.

## When Splitwise is the better choice

- You share a house and split rent, bills and the weekly shop.
- You're tracking a whole trip: the villa, car hire, fuel, supermarket runs.
- Your group already uses Splitwise, and you'd rather keep every debt in one place.
- Some of your group want an app of their own on Android.
- You need multiple currencies.

## When OnlyBills is the better choice

- It's a UK restaurant or pub bill with service charge and shared dishes.
- You want each friend to pay you directly to your own bank, by link.
- Some friends won't tap anything, so you need to assign their items yourself.
- Your group doesn't use Splitwise and you don't want to move everyone into it.

## Using both together

They work well side by side. On a group holiday, a sensible setup is:

1. Keep a Splitwise group for the trip's shared costs.
2. At each big dinner, split the bill by item (in either app) so everyone pays for what they had.
3. If you'd rather settle everything at the end, put each person's dinner share into the Splitwise group.

The same works for housemates: Splitwise for the house, an itemised split for the night out. For trips in particular, see [how to split costs on a group holiday](/blog/split-bill-on-holiday/).

## FAQ

### Is OnlyBills a Splitwise replacement?
Not for running balances. OnlyBills splits individual bills by item and doesn't track ongoing shared costs like rent. If that's what you need, Splitwise (or Tricount, or Splid) is the better fit.

### Can Splitwise split a receipt by item?
Yes. Itemize, launched in the US and UK in October 2026, lets one person photograph a receipt and friends claim their items by scanning a QR code, without the app or an account. It's free.

### Do friends need an account for OnlyBills?
No. Friends join a live table from a link or QR code in their browser, or an App Clip on iPhone, type their name and tick their items. Only the host needs the app.

### Does OnlyBills work in other currencies?
No, it works in pounds. For trips abroad with spending in several currencies, a balance app like Splitwise or Splid handles conversion.
