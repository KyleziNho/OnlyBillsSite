To split a bill on Venmo, pay the check, then tap the blue V button, choose **Split**, pick your friends and enter the total or each person's amount. Venmo sends everyone a request. If you paid with your Venmo debit card or Venmo checkout, you can split straight from the transaction instead, and Venmo's new receipt scanning (rolling out since November 2026) can assign items for you.

The catch is that every friend needs a Venmo account to pay. Below: each way to split, how to get uneven amounts right with tax and tip, and what to do about the friend who isn't on Venmo. We checked the steps against Venmo's help pages on 14 November 2026. Menus move, so if a button has a slightly different name, that's why.

## Three ways to split on Venmo

| You paid with | How to split | Uneven amounts? |
|---|---|---|
| Your Venmo debit card or Venmo checkout | From the transaction: **Wallet**, then **Transactions**, then **Split** | Yes, custom amounts |
| Any other card, or cash | Blue V button, then **Split**, enter the amount | Yes, custom amounts |
| Any card, and you have the receipt | Blue V button, then **Scan receipt** | Yes, by item |

Every option ends the same way: each friend gets a request, and the money lands in your Venmo account once they accept it.

## How to split a purchase you made on Venmo

If the dinner went on your Venmo Mastercard debit card or through Venmo checkout:

1. Open the **Wallet** tab and choose **Transactions**.
2. Select the purchase and tap **Split**.
3. Choose who to include. You're added automatically, and you can remove yourself.
4. Pick an even split or custom amounts, and add a tip or other charges if you need to.
5. Add a note, then send and confirm the requests.

Splitting doesn't change what you paid. It just creates requests for your friends' shares.

## How to split a bill you paid with another card

If you paid with any other card, or cash:

1. Tap the blue **V** button and choose **Split**.
2. Choose the people to split with.
3. Enter the total, or set a custom amount for each person. Swipe left on your own name to leave yourself out.
4. Tap **Request**, add a note, tap **Next** to review, then **Request** again.

## Venmo's receipt scanning (new in November 2026)

On 9 November 2026 Venmo announced receipt scanning. You photograph or upload the receipt, Venmo lists the items, you assign each one to whoever ordered it, and Venmo adds everyone's share of tax and tip and sends the requests. To split something evenly, assign it to "Everyone".

Venmo says it is "rolling out now, and will be widely available in the coming weeks", so you may not see **Scan receipt** under the blue V button yet. You may also need to give Venmo access to your camera and photos.

If everyone at the table uses Venmo, this is now the simplest way to split an uneven check without doing the math yourself. What Venmo's announcement doesn't spell out is whether friends without Venmo can be included. Requests are paid in Venmo, so assume they'll need an account.

## How to request different amounts from each person

Without the receipt feature, you work out each person's share and type it in. Here's a real-looking example.

Four of you have dinner. The food and drinks come to $116.00. Sales tax is 7.25% (California's statewide base rate; your city's will differ) and you tip 20% on the pre-tax amount.

- Tax: $8.41
- Tip: $23.20
- Total: $147.61, on your card

Each person pays their own items plus the same share of tax and tip:

| Person | Ordered | Share of tax and tip | Owes |
|---|---|---|---|
| You | $31.50 | $8.58 | $40.08 (you paid) |
| Maya | $18.00 | $4.91 | $22.91 |
| Jordan | $42.00 | $11.44 | $53.44 |
| Sam | $24.50 | $6.68 | $31.18 |
| **Total** | **$116.00** | **$31.61** | **$147.61** |

In Venmo, choose custom amounts and enter $22.91, $53.44 and $31.18. Split evenly, everyone would pay $36.90, so Maya would cover almost $14 of other people's dinner.

The quick formula: **your share = your items × (total ÷ subtotal)**. Here that's × 1.2725. The full method, including shared appetizers and the last cent, is in [how to split a check with tax and tip](/blog/split-check-tax-and-tip/).

## What if a friend doesn't have Venmo?

They can't pay a Venmo request without signing up. Rather than push them onto a new app, give them another way:

- **Zelle:** if their bank app has Zelle, they send to your phone number or email. There's no Zelle link to send, so they type your details in. More in [can you split a bill with Zelle?](/blog/split-bill-zelle/)
- **Cash App payment link:** if you have Cash App, a request sent as a link can be paid with Apple Pay or Google Pay, no Cash App account needed. See [how to split a bill on Cash App](/blog/split-bill-cash-app/).
- **PayPal:** PayPal users in the US can now send money to a Venmo account (they search for the phone number on your Venmo). You can't request across the two, so they have to send it.

If your group is a mix of all of these, [how to split a bill when everyone uses a different payment app](/blog/split-bill-different-payment-apps/) has one message that covers everyone.

## Fees and limits

Sending money to friends on Venmo is free from a Venmo balance, a linked bank account or a debit card. Paying with a credit card costs 3%. Moving money out of Venmo to your bank is free with a standard transfer, or 1.75% for an Instant Transfer (minimum $0.25, maximum $25).

Nobody pays anything to send or receive a request. The person paying chooses how to fund it.

## FAQ

### Can you split a bill on Venmo with someone who doesn't have Venmo?
Not with a Venmo request. Paying one needs a Venmo account. Send that friend another way to pay you, such as Zelle from their bank app, a Cash App payment link (payable with Apple Pay or Google Pay) or PayPal. PayPal users in the US can now send money to a Venmo account, though requests don't cross between the two.

### Can Venmo split a bill unevenly?
Yes. When you split, choose custom amounts instead of an even split and type each person's share. Venmo's receipt scanning, rolling out since November 2026, lets you assign items to people instead.

### Does Venmo charge to split a bill?
No fee to send from your Venmo balance, bank account or debit card. Paying with a credit card costs 3%, and that fee falls on the person paying, not the person requesting.

### Does Venmo add tax and tip when you split?
Venmo says its receipt scanning adds each person's share of tax and tip. With a manual split you add it yourself. The fair way is in proportion to what each person ordered.

### How do I get paid back on Venmo without chasing people?
Send the requests before you leave the table, one per person, with what they had in the note. People pay a specific, itemized request faster than a vague "dinner" one.
