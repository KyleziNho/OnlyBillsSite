The best bill splitting app depends on the job. For a house or a two week trip, pick a balance app: Tricount if you want it free with no limits, Splitwise if your friends already use it, Settle Up if you want a web app too. For one restaurant or pub bill where everyone had different things, pick an app that reads the receipt and lets each person claim their own items: Splitwise's free Itemize feature or OnlyBills. If everyone had about the same and you bank with Monzo, Revolut or Starling, the split button in your bank's app is enough.

Below is what each one does, what's free, whether you need an account, and which phones it works on. We checked every fact on the app's own website, its UK App Store listing or launch coverage, as of November 2026. Prices and features change, so check the listing before you pay for anything.

## Which bill splitting app should you use?

Start with what you're splitting, not with the app.

| You're splitting | Good fit | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Rent, bills and the food shop with flatmates | Tricount, Splitwise, Settle Up | They keep a running balance per group for months |
| A holiday or stag do | Tricount, Splitwise, Settle Up | Many costs, several payers, often more than one currency |
| One restaurant or pub bill, everyone had different things | OnlyBills, Splitwise Itemize | They read the receipt so each person pays for what they had |
| One card payment, split evenly | Monzo, Revolut or Starling | Free, already on your phone, sends payment requests |

Plenty of groups use two: a balance app for the trip or the flat, and an itemised split for the big dinners.

## Splitwise

Splitwise is the best known of the lot. You add expenses as they happen, split them equally, by amounts, percentages or shares, and it keeps a running balance per group. It runs on iPhone, Android and the web.

In mid October 2026 Splitwise launched Itemize in the US and UK. One person photographs the receipt, the others scan a QR code and claim their own items without downloading the app or creating an account, and it's free for all Splitwise users (The Paypers, 16 October 2026).

**Free or paid:** free, with a daily cap on how many expenses you can add. Splitwise Pro lists "unlimited expenses", an ad-free experience, receipt scanning and currency conversion among its features. The UK App Store lists Pro purchase options from £2.79 to £47.99.

**Suits:** a flat or a regular group who'll keep using it. For how it handles one restaurant bill next to OnlyBills, see [OnlyBills vs Splitwise](/vs/splitwise/).

## Tricount

Tricount, owned by the Dutch bank bunq since 2022, sells itself on price: "Free. No ads. No limits." You make a group, add who paid for what, and it shows balances and who should pay whom. Its UK App Store listing mentions over 100 currencies, offline use, payment requests, sharing what friends owe you "without needing them to be on the app", and importing your Splitwise groups. Its website says you can try it without signing up, and it's on iPhone and Android.

Neither its website nor its listing mentions reading a receipt line by line. You add each expense as an amount and choose who it's for.

**Suits:** trips and households who want a free balance app with no subscription. More on it in [Tricount alternatives](/blog/tricount-alternatives/).

## Settle Up

Settle Up tracks shared costs for trips, flatmates and couples. Its App Store listing says it supports all currencies with live exchange rates, works offline and syncs later, and shows who should pay next with the fewest transfers. Group members can see balances in a browser through a shared link, and there's a web app as well as iPhone and Android apps.

**Free or paid:** the free version has ads. Premium removes them and adds receipt photos, recurring expenses, statistics and Excel export. On the UK App Store, Premium is £3.99 a month or £18.99 a year, and a whole group can be upgraded from £5.99 for a week.

**Suits:** groups who want stats, recurring expenses and a web version.

## Monzo, Revolut and Starling

If you paid on your card and the split is even, your bank may already do the job, free.

- **Monzo Split:** split a one-off bill or keep a Running Split for ongoing costs. Monzo says friends can join as guests from a link "even if they don't live in the UK or bank with us", and that Split is "completely free, for you and all your friends."
- **Revolut:** the Split bill button is for other Revolut customers. Group Bills lets you invite people who don't bank with Revolut, through a website.
- **Starling:** Split the Bill sends IOUs, and friends who aren't with Starling pay through a Settle Up link on a secure web page.

They split a total, not a receipt, so they can't tell who had the steak. Step by step guides are in [how to split a bill with Monzo, Revolut and Starling](/blog/split-bill-monzo-revolut-starling/).

## OnlyBills

OnlyBills is a UK app built for one job: splitting a restaurant, pub or group bill by what each person had. The host scans the receipt and it reads every line. You check it adds up, then either share a live table, where friends scan a QR code or open a link and tick their own items, or assign the items yourself.

- **Friends don't download anything.** The table opens in their browser, or as an [App Clip](/blog/what-is-an-app-clip/) on iPhone, and they see their share add up as they tick.
- **Shared things split fairly.** A bottle of wine splits between the people who drank it, and service charge, tips and discounts are spread in proportion. Shares are exact to the penny and add up to the bill.
- **Getting paid.** Friends pay the host with a Monzo link (card, Apple Pay or bank transfer, no Monzo account needed), a Starling or Revolut link (card or Apple Pay, no account needed), PayPal (a PayPal account may be needed) or bank transfer from any banking app.
- **Owed** keeps a running balance with each friend across bills, and you can add IOUs for the taxi or the tickets.

**Free or paid:** free, with no sign-up for anyone, host included. Free includes 30 receipt scans in any 30 days. Typing a bill in is always unlimited. An optional subscription, OnlyBills Pro, raises the scan limit.

**Not for:** rent and household bills, or a whole trip's costs. And the host needs an iPhone or iPad: there's no Android app, although friends on Android can join a table from their browser.

## Does itemising make a real difference?

On an uneven bill, yes. Four friends, £96 of food and drink plus 12.5% service (£12), so £108 in total.

| Person | Ordered | Equal split | Fair share (with service) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Asha | £36.00 | £27.00 | £40.50 |
| Ben | £28.00 | £27.00 | £31.50 |
| Cara | £12.00 | £27.00 | £13.50 |
| Dan | £20.00 | £27.00 | £22.50 |

Split equally, Cara pays £13.50 more than her share and Asha pays £13.50 less. Every app on this page can do the equal split. Only the itemised ones get Cara the right number without someone working it out on a napkin. Or do the sum yourself with the free calculator:

## Do your friends need the app too?

It varies, and it's the thing that most often stops a split happening.

- **Splitwise:** with Itemize, friends can claim from a QR code without the app or an account. For ongoing groups, people normally have the app.
- **Tricount:** you can share what people owe you without them being on the app.
- **Settle Up:** friends can view balances in a browser from a link.
- **Your bank:** Monzo and Starling friends at other banks pay by link. Revolut's Split bill button needs Revolut on both sides.
- **OnlyBills:** friends join from a QR code or link, no download or account.

For more on paying by link and what each method shares about you, see [pay your share without downloading an app](/blog/pay-without-downloading-app/).

## FAQ

### What is the best free bill splitting app in the UK?
For ongoing costs, Tricount says it's free with no ads and no limits. For one restaurant bill split by item, OnlyBills is free (30 receipt scans in any 30 days, typing a bill in unlimited) and Splitwise's Itemize is free. Monzo Split is free for Monzo customers.

### Which bill splitting apps work on Android?
Splitwise, Tricount, Settle Up and the Monzo, Revolut and Starling apps are all on Android. OnlyBills is iPhone and iPad only, but friends with Android phones can join an OnlyBills table from their browser.

### Do I need to sign up to split a bill?
Not always. Tricount lets you try it without signing up, and OnlyBills needs no account for anyone. Splitwise needs an account for the person using the app, and bank split features need an account with that bank.

### Which app is best for splitting a restaurant bill?
One that reads the receipt and lets people claim what they had: Splitwise Itemize or OnlyBills. If everyone had about the same, an equal split from your bank's app is quicker.

### What's the best app for flatmates?
A balance app that keeps a running total over months, such as Splitwise, Tricount or Settle Up. OnlyBills isn't built for rent or household bills.
