Send the bill to the group chat the same night, in one message, with what it was for, what each person owes and a way to pay. If people had different things, send a link where everyone ticks their own items instead of a list of amounts you had to work out. The faster and clearer the message, the fewer people you'll need to chase.

It's worth getting right. In a 2023 Opinium survey of 2,000 UK adults for Starling Bank, 32% said they'd had to ask someone for money back two or more times, and the average wait to be paid back was more than two months (Starling Bank, 2023).

:::stat 32% | have had to ask someone two or more times to be paid back | Starling Bank / Opinium, 2023

## When should you send the bill?

Before everyone gets home. Straight after the meal, or on the train back, is ideal.

| When | How it lands |
|---|---|
| At the table | Normal, and people can pay before they leave |
| Same evening | Still part of the night out |
| Next morning | Fine. "Sorting last night" |
| A week later | People have to remember what they had |

If you're still at the table, it's often easier to show the bill on your phone or share a QR code than to post in the chat. The group chat is best when people have already left, or when not everyone was there for the whole meal.

## What should the message say?

Four things, in this order:

1. **What it was for.** "Dinner at Saffron House."
2. **The total, with service.** So nobody wonders if it's missing.
3. **Each person's share, or how they'll find it.** Exact amounts, not "about £30".
4. **How to pay.** A payment link or your bank details, in the same message.

Leave out the apology and the long explanation. If someone wants to check your maths, a photo of the receipt does that.

## Message templates you can copy

### Everyone splits equally

> Thanks for tonight! Saffron House came to £129.60 with service, so £32.40 each. My Monzo link: [link]

### People had different things

Work out each share first, including their part of the service charge. [Splitting service charge fairly](/blog/split-service-charge-fairly/) shows how.

> Here's Saffron House by what everyone had, service included: Sam £38.70, Priya £31.05, Jack £34.20, Maya £25.65. Pay here when you can: [link]

Check the shares add up to the bill before you send. Those four come to £129.60.

### You want people to tick their own

> Tap what you had at Saffron House and it'll tell you your share: [link]

### Someone left early

> Hope you got back OK! Your bit of tonight was £25.65 (pasta, a glass of red and your part of the service). Link: [link]

### Bank transfer instead of a link

> Dinner came to £32.40 each. Bank details: Alex Smith, 12-34-56, 12345678. Thanks!

Put your name exactly as your bank has it, so the name check matches when they pay. Not sure about posting your details to a big group? See [is it safe to share your bank details](/blog/is-it-safe-to-share-bank-details/).

:::note
Put the amount near the start. People read messages in the notification preview, and "£32.40" is the bit they need.
:::

## What does an OnlyBills link look like in the group chat?

With OnlyBills, the person who paid scans the receipt, checks it adds up and shares a live table. **Send invite** opens your phone's share sheet with a ready-made message ("Tap what you had at Saffron House:" and the link), so you can drop it straight into the group chat.

In iMessage, the link shows as a preview with the restaurant's name and photo, "Tap what you had" and onlybills.app underneath.

:::imessage Saffron House · Tap what you had | onlybills.app | An illustration of the link in iMessage. The real preview shows the venue's name, its photo when there is one, and onlybills.app underneath.

This is what makes it less awkward to send. Instead of a friend telling everyone what they owe, the group gets a tidy, neutral bill from the meal. It's still a link you sent, and it says onlybills.app, but nobody has to read "you owe me" from you. They just open it and pick their own items.

A few details, so you know what to expect:

- **The photo isn't always there.** If OnlyBills has no photo for the venue, the preview shows the OnlyBills icon instead, with the restaurant's name.
- **If the venue name wasn't read**, the title is simply "Tap what you had".
- **Other apps draw their own previews.** WhatsApp and most other messaging apps read the same kind of page details to build a preview, but each lays it out its own way, and some may show a plainer version. The link works the same either way.
- **Now and then you may get a plain preview** with the OnlyBills icon and "Your share of the bill". That happens if the venue details can't be fetched quickly enough. The link still opens the right table.

## What do friends see when they tap the link?

They don't need to download anything. On iPhone, the link can open as an App Clip, a small part of the app that runs without installing it. On Android, or anywhere else, it opens in the phone's browser. There's more on how that works in [what is an App Clip](/blog/what-is-an-app-clip/).

:::shot guest-01-join | Joining the table at Saffron House: type your name, or tap That's me if you're already listed.

Then:

1. They see the restaurant's name, who's hosting and how many people are at the table, and type their name.
2. They tick what they had. A shared dish splits between everyone who ticks it.
3. Their share adds up as they go, with their part of the service charge.
4. They tap the pay button and pay you from the same screen.

:::shot guest-05-live | Your share stays up to date as other people claim their items.

How they pay depends on the link you've set up. Monzo takes card, Apple Pay or bank transfer, with no Monzo account needed. Starling and Revolut take card or Apple Pay, with no account needed. PayPal may need a PayPal account, and bank transfer works from any banking app. The money goes to you, not to OnlyBills. When they've paid, they tap "I've paid" and you see them ticked off on your phone.

The guest side is walked through step by step in [claim your items without the app](/how-to/claim-your-items-without-the-app/). Setting up your side is in [share a live table](/how-to/share-a-live-table/).

## How do you follow up without being awkward?

Give it 3 to 5 days, then send one short, friendly reminder. Shorter than the first message, not longer.

> Just bumping the Saffron House link in case it got buried: [link] 🙏

If one person is left, message them on their own rather than in the group. Being chased in front of everyone is the awkward part.

> Hey, did you see the link from Saturday? Your bit was £34.20. No rush, just didn't want it to get lost.

Asking "did you see it?" gives them an easy way to say "oh, sorry, doing it now". If that doesn't work, the next steps are in [what to do when someone won't pay their share](/blog/someone-wont-pay-their-share/). And to head it off next time, see [how to avoid an awkward bill split](/blog/avoid-awkward-bill-split/).

In OnlyBills, anyone who hasn't paid when you finish the table goes onto your Owed page, and you get a reminder after a week to chase them. Friends can still pay from the same link for 7 days after you finish.

## FAQ

### Should you send the bill in the group chat or to each person?
The group chat for the first message, so everyone sees the same total and link at once. Reminders are better sent to the person on their own.

### How soon after dinner should you send the bill?
The same evening, ideally before people get home. The next morning is fine too. After a week it starts to feel like chasing.

### Do my friends need the OnlyBills app to use the link?
No. Only the person who paid needs the app. Everyone else opens the link in their browser, or as an App Clip on iPhone, ticks what they had and pays.

### Why does the link show the restaurant's photo?
OnlyBills uses the venue name and, when it has one, a photo of the venue for the link preview, so everyone can see which meal the bill is for. If there's no photo, it shows the OnlyBills icon.
