In Spain, friends split a bill with Bizum: one person pays the restaurant, then everyone sends them their share by Bizum, a phone-number payment built into Spanish banking apps. The person who paid can also send a **request** to each friend for their amount. Bizum moves the money straight away, but it doesn't work out who owes what, and it only works if you bank in Spain.

So if you're living in Spain, studying there or just visiting, here's how it works, what to do when you're the one without Bizum, and a worked example.

## What is Bizum?

Bizum is Spain's way of paying friends by phone number. It lives inside the banking app of most Spanish banks rather than as a separate app, and it had 30.6 million users at the end of 2025, according to Bizum's figures reported by Infobae. You link your mobile number to your Spanish bank account once, then anyone with Bizum can pay you by typing that number.

That's why, at the end of a dinner in Madrid or Valencia, you'll hear "te hago un Bizum" (I'll Bizum you). Nobody swaps account numbers.

## How a group splits the bill with Bizum

1. One person pays the whole bill by card.
2. They work out each person's share.
3. Either everyone sends them a Bizum, or they open Bizum in their banking app, tap **Solicitar** (request), pick friends and type each amount.
4. Each friend accepts the request and the money arrives straight away.

Many banking apps let you request from several people at once, up to 30 according to bank guides, and some let you set a different amount for each. A few split a total evenly for you. None of them read the receipt.

## A worked example

Six friends have dinner. The bill is €148.50 and Ana pays it.

| Person | Main | Drink | Shared bread, croquetas, bravas | Owes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ana (paid) | €16.00 | wine €6.00 | €4.50 | €26.50 |
| Bea | €14.00 | soft drink €2.50 | €4.50 | €21.00 |
| Carlos | €19.00 | wine €6.00 | €4.50 | €29.50 |
| Dani | €12.00 | water €2.00 | €4.50 | €18.50 |
| Elena | €15.00 | wine €6.00 | €4.50 | €25.50 |
| Fer | €17.00 | wine €6.00 | €4.50 | €27.50 |

The two bottles of wine (€24) are split between the four who drank it. An even split would be €24.75 each, which would leave Dani paying €6.25 for wine he didn't drink.

Tipping in Spain is optional and modest: people round up or leave a few euros. If you add one, share it in proportion to what each person spent.

## If you don't have Bizum

This is the usual situation for visitors and new arrivals. Bizum needs an account at a Spanish bank that offers it. Some app banks offer it too: N26 says Bizum is available to customers with an account opened in Spain, and Revolut has a Bizum page for customers in Spain. Check your app before you count on it.

If you can't use Bizum, you have three ways to pay a Spanish friend back:

- **Bank transfer to their IBAN.** Since October 2025, eurozone banks have had to offer instant euro transfers and a free payee name check, so a transfer from a French, German or Irish account arrives almost immediately. Ask for their name exactly as the bank has it.
- **A payment link.** If your friend has Revolut or PayPal, a Revolut.me or PayPal.me link is often easiest. You can pay a Revolut.me link by card without a Revolut account.
- **Cash.** Still fine in Spain, if you have it.

If you're the one who paid and your Spanish friends want to pay you, the same applies in reverse. They can't Bizum a UK or US number, so give them your IBAN or a payment link. A UK account can receive euros by international transfer, but your bank may charge a fee and convert at its own rate.

## Bizum limits

Bizum sets overall limits and each bank can set lower ones. The figures repeated across Spanish banks' guides are €0.50 to €1,000 per payment (many banks cap it at €500), around €2,000 received per day, and 60 payments received per month. A dinner won't get near any of them, but a big group booking might. Sending money to friends is normally free; your bank's app has the exact limits.

## FAQ

### What is Bizum?
Bizum is Spain's instant payment by phone number. It's built into most Spanish banking apps: you link your mobile number to your account, and friends pay you by typing it in. Money arrives straight away. It had 30.6 million users at the end of 2025.

### Can a tourist use Bizum?
Usually not. Bizum needs an account with a Spanish bank or app bank that offers it, such as N26 for customers with an account opened in Spain. Visitors normally pay friends back by IBAN transfer, a Revolut.me or PayPal.me link, or cash.

### How much can you send with Bizum?
Between €0.50 and €1,000 per payment under Bizum's general rules, but many banks set a lower cap such as €500, plus daily and monthly limits. Check your banking app for your exact limits.

### How do I pay a Spanish friend back if I don't have Bizum?
Ask for their IBAN and name as it appears on the account. Eurozone instant transfers arrive almost immediately. If they have Revolut or PayPal, a payment link works too, and a Revolut.me link can be paid by card without an account.

### Does Bizum work with Portugal and Italy?
At some banks, yes. Bizum connects with MB WAY in Portugal and Bancomat in Italy, and Santander was the first Spanish bank to offer it, in 2025. It depends on your bank. A wider link to 13 European countries was agreed in February 2026.
